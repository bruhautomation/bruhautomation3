# UniFi Camera Mount

A printed bracket that puts a Ubiquiti UniFi camera where its own wall plate
won't go — vinyl siding, a soffit, or any surface that isn't flat, solid and
plumb.

## What's in this folder

Nothing but the guide yet. The bracket geometry has not been exported, so
`index.mdx` carries `pending: true` and the page is flagged on the site. What
the guide does cover is the part list and the install, which is the half that
decides whether a camera stays up and gives usable night video.

## Hardware

- 3D-printed adapter plate — the camera's **own** supplied bracket bolts to it.
  Don't reproduce Ubiquiti's mounting interface in plastic; a printed copy of a
  ball joint creeps in the sun until the frame is all gutter
- PETG, not PLA. A PLA bracket on a sunlit wall droops slowly over a summer
- M3 brass heat-set inserts, so the camera can come off and go back on more
  than twice
- Stainless M3 hardware — the black grade-10.9 kits weep rust down white siding
- Outdoor-rated / direct-burial Cat cable, and a PoE injector or a PoE switch
  for a G6 Turret. A G3-Micro is WiFi and takes a USB lead instead
- Vinyl siding hooks, or a siding mounting block with a built-in box, depending
  on how permanent the install is
