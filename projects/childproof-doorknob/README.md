# Childproof Doorknob

A printed slip-ring cover for Schlage Bowery knobs. It goes on behind the knob
during installation and is trapped there when the knob clicks onto the spindle,
so there is nothing for a child to pull off. Squeeze through the two side
windows to grip the knob and turn; grab it anywhere else and it just spins.

**Build guide:** [bruhautomation.com/projects/childproof-doorknob](https://bruhautomation.com/projects/childproof-doorknob/)

## What's in this folder

- `models/` — print-ready STL (this is what the site previews)
- `cad/` — STEP export for remixing
- `images/` — photos of the knob, the cover and the install

## The part

- 63 mm outside diameter, 40.5 mm tall, 3 mm wall
- Ø23.2 mm collar bore, sleeves over the knob's neck
- Two opposed windows in the wall, roughly 30 × 14 mm, starting 4.5 mm below the rim
- A 10 × 6.6 mm notch in the collar's door-side edge, clearing the knob's release slot
- About 20 mL of resin, or roughly 25 g of filament

## Hardware

- Schlage F10 Bowery passage knob with Greyson trim, matte black — the cover fits
  this knob and no other Schlage model
- Black water-washable 405 nm resin (MSLA), or plain black PLA/PETG on FDM.
  Not carbon-filled — it is abrasive against the knob's paint
