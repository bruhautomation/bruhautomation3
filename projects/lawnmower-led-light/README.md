# Lawnmower LED Light

Two 7-inch off-road LED pods wired onto a riding mower so the last hour of
daylight stops being the last hour of mowing.

No microcontroller and nothing to flash — it is 12 V DC wiring done properly:
fused at the battery, switched through a 30 A relay so the dash switch never
carries the light current, sealed at every joint with solder-seal butt
connectors, and routed clear of the belts, the muffler and the deck's travel.

The pods mount to the hood or the front frame, never to the deck. The two things
that bite: the wattage printed on the pods is fiction, so measure the real draw
before you pick a fuse; and a mower's charging system is sized to recover from
cranking, not to run accessories, so keep a maintainer on the battery.
