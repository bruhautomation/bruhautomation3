# Dinner Bowl Rest

A printed cradle that a dinner bowl drops into, so it can't spin, slide or tip
while you whisk in it or scrape it out. A bowl with a rounded foot touches the
counter in one small circle; anything done one-handed makes it move.

## Hardware
- 3D-printed ring, PETG — not PLA, which softens under a hot bowl
- Three grip pads on the underside: printed TPU, or self-adhesive rubber bumpers

## Sizing
The bore is sized to the outside diameter of your bowl's foot, plus 0.3–0.5 mm
of clearance. Make it a shallow cone rather than a cylinder and one rest catches
every bowl in the cupboard.
