# Tablet Wall Mount

A flush magnetic tablet mount built on a printed keystone wall plate. The
charging cable stays inside the wall, a 40 lb studded magnet holds the tablet,
and the printed keystone insert the magnet bolts to comes in four angles, so the
same plate leans the tablet out at 0°, 10°, 15° or 20°.

**Build guide:** [bruhautomation.com/projects/tablet-wall-mount](https://bruhautomation.com/projects/tablet-wall-mount/)

## What's in this folder

- `models/` — print-ready STL files (these are what the site previews)
- `cad/` — STEP exports for both plates, the jack insert and both bezels
- `preview/` — preview meshes tessellated from the CAD (generated; the site's 3D viewer loads these)
- `images/` — photos

### The parts

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `keystone_plate_1_gang.stl` | 88.90 × 133.35 × 9.80 mm jumbo 1-gang plate; keystone port plus a 7.5 mm cable slot |
| `keystone_plate_2_gang.stl` | Wide version; 6.00 mm M6 stud hole straight through, no insert, no angle |
| `0/10/15/20_degree_keystone.stl` | Keystone inserts, 14.50 × 16.00 mm face, 6.60 mm hole for the magnet's M6 stud |
| `20_degree_keystone_jack.stl` | The 20° insert with the body extended to 42 mm |
| `vizio_xr6m10_bezel_1_gang.stl` | Full bezel surround for a VIZIO XR6M10, 95 × 195 × 25.55 mm |
| `vizio_xr6m10_bezel_2_gang.stl` | Same bezel on a dual-gang bracket, 161.79 × 195 × 25.55 mm |

## Hardware

- 1-gang low-voltage mounting bracket
- 40 lb rubber-coated neodymium magnet with an M6 male stud, plus an M6 nut
- Stick-on steel plate for the back of the tablet
- Short right-angle USB cable, and a long one to run down the wall cavity
- Two #6-32 wall plate screws
