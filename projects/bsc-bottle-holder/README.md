# BSC Bottle Holder

A printed rack that stands media bottles up along one side of a biosafety
cabinet's deck, clear of the front and rear grilles that carry the downflow.

**Build guide:** [bruhautomation.com/projects/bsc-bottle-holder](https://bruhautomation.com/projects/bsc-bottle-holder/)

## Hardware
- 3D-printed bottle holder structure — PETG, not PLA: it gets alcohol-wiped
  daily and often sits under a germicidal UV lamp overnight
- TPU printed feet, or self-adhesive rubber bumpers, so it doesn't skate on the
  stainless deck
- Stainless M3 hardware and heat-set inserts, only if the model is split to fit
  the bed

Not autoclavable (PETG softens around 80 °C) and not sterilisable — an FDM part
is porous. It holds closed bottles and nothing else.
