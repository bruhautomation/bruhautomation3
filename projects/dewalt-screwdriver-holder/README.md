# DeWalt Screwdriver Holder

A printed wall mount for the DeWalt GYRO screwdriver — a 1.5 in square plate
with a 20 mm sleeve on it, fixed with two screws.

**Build guide:** [bruhautomation.com/projects/dewalt-screwdriver-holder](https://bruhautomation.com/projects/dewalt-screwdriver-holder/)

## What's in this folder

- `cad/` — Fusion 360 sources (`.f3d`) and STEP exports
- `models/` — the printable STL

## The part

- Base plate 38.1 × 38.1 mm (1.5 in square), 1 mm thick, 6 mm corner radii
- Sleeve Ø24 mm outside, Ø20 mm bore, 20 mm long, 2 mm wall, 1 mm lead-in radius at both ends
- Overall height 25 mm
- Two Ø3 mm mounting holes on the centreline, 28.1 mm apart, each with a Ø8 mm × 0.75 mm head relief

## Hardware

- 3D-printed mount (PLA+ indoors, PETG for a garage)
- 2 × #4 × 3/8 in wood screws, or M3 screws with washers through a panel
- DeWalt GYRO screwdriver — the tool this is sized around, not part of the build
