# Magnetic Ring Unlocker

Opens a childproof magnetic cabinet lock with a ring you wear. Magnetic locks
ship with one plastic key fob that lives on top of the fridge; this carries the
same magnet on your hand instead, so the cupboard opens with the hand already
reaching for it.

**Build guide:** [bruhautomation.com/projects/magnetic-ring-unlocker](https://bruhautomation.com/projects/magnetic-ring-unlocker/)

## What's in this folder

Nothing printable yet — the model still has to be exported. The build guide
covers the parts and the sizing in the meantime.

## How it works

The lock is a spring-loaded steel latch on the inside of the cabinet door. A
strong enough magnet held against the outside of the door front pulls the latch
clear; take it away and the spring puts it back. The key never touches the lock,
so there is no fit to get right — only enough field to reach through roughly
3/4 in of door.

## Hardware

- A neodymium magnet with a wide pole face. A countersunk cup magnet screws
  straight to the ring and needs no glue; a plain disc about an inch across, with
  a steel plate behind it, is what the factory key uses and reaches slightly
  further
- PETG or PLA+ for the band — a couple of grams a ring
- The magnetic cabinet locks themselves, if the house doesn't already have them

## Safety

Two swallowed neodymium magnets are a surgical emergency. This is a project built
because there is a toddler in the house, and it puts a strong magnet within reach
by design — the bond between magnet and ring is the safety-critical part, and the
rest of the pack belongs somewhere a child cannot get to.
