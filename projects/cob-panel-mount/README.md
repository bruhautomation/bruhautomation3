# COB Panel Mount

A printed bracket for the cheap chip-on-board LED panels — the bare 12 V, 70 W,
8.6 × 4.4 in aluminium boards sold as flood-bulb retrofits, with no housing, no
driver and no way to hang them.

The bracket carries the panel and its heatsink stack. It never touches the LED
board itself, and it is printed in PETG rather than PLA, because it bolts to
aluminium that runs at 60–70 °C.

## Hardware

- 12 V 70 W COB panel, 8.6 × 4.4 in, 6500 K
- 8 × aluminium heatsink, 100 × 25 × 10 mm, bonded across the back with thermal
  glue
- CC-CV buck converter fed from a 24 V supply — the panel has no driver in it,
  and a fixed-voltage supply will run it into thermal runaway
- M3 heat-set inserts and screws; #8 wood screws or wall anchors to mount it

The build guide, the wiring and the current-setting procedure are in
`index.mdx`.
