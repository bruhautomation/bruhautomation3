# Christmas Ornaments

Smart-home themed ornaments, printed as hollow shells with room inside for a
light. Three ways to light one: a flameless LED tea light dropped in, a 12 mm
WS2811 bullet pixel pressed into the shell so the ornament joins the tree's
existing string, or a WS2812 ring and a D1 mini so it becomes its own device in
Home Assistant.

## Hardware
- Matte white PLA for anything lit from inside; glow-in-the-dark PLA for the ones
  with no electronics at all
- A hardened nozzle — glow filament is abrasive
- Optional: flameless tea lights, WS2811 bullet pixels, or a WS2812 ring with a
  Wemos D1 mini
- 6 × 2 mm magnets if the shell should open to change a cell
