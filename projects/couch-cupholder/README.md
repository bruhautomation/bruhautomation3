# Couch Cupholder

A two-part printed saddle that drops over the arm of a couch and holds a drink.
No fasteners, no glue, no strap — the arch grips the arm and the insert drops
into the pocket.

**Build guide:** [bruhautomation.com/projects/couch-cupholder](https://bruhautomation.com/projects/couch-cupholder/)

## Fit

The arch is 85 mm deep and pinches from 193 mm at its widest to about 187 mm at
the mouth. It suits a couch arm 180–190 mm (7–7½ in) across and takes up 93 mm
of the arm's length. Measure before printing; there is no adjustment.

## What's in this folder

- `models/` — print-ready STLs (these are what the site previews)
  - `bruh_couch_arm_cupholder.stl` — the saddle. 206 × 93 × 141 mm
  - `bruh_couch_arm_cupholder_insert.stl` — the drop-in cup well. 101 × 91 × 48 mm
- `cad/` — STEP exports, for editing the design

## Dimensions

- Pocket: 103 × 93 mm obround, 50 mm deep, 4 mm wall, 6 mm floor
- Insert: same obround 1 mm smaller all round, 78 mm bore, 45 mm well depth
- Skirts: 6 mm wall

## Hardware

None. Two printed parts, printed in carbon-fibre PLA or PLA+. A handful of
self-adhesive rubber bumpers inside the skirts is the only optional extra.
