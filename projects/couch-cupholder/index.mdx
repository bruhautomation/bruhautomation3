## Design

A cupholder that slips over the arm of the couch. It includes swappable inserts for different cup sizes. Printed in carbon fiber PLA for a clean surface finish and solid feel.

## Make Your Own

<AffiliateDisclosure />

Printed on an [Ender 3 V2](https://amzn.to/3ZfeX4p) using [carbon fiber PLA](https://amzn.to/48qm3aL). STL and STEP files are in the [GitHub repo](https://github.com/bruhautomation/bruhautomation3/tree/main/projects/couch-cupholder) — remix to fit your couch arm and cup sizes.
