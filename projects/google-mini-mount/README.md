# Google Mini Mount

Wall mount for the first-generation Google Home Mini. A printed cradle screws to
the wall and holds the puck at head height, with a short right-angle cable
dropping into a raceway so nothing trails down the paint.

The Nest Mini that replaced it in 2019 has a wall-mount hole in its base already
and does not need this.

## Hardware

- 3D-printed mounting bracket
- Drywall anchors and screws (or a #8 wood screw where there's a stud)
- Right-angle micro-USB cable, 1 ft, plus a USB wall charger
- Cord raceway or adhesive cable clips
- Self-adhesive rubber bumpers for the inside of the cradle
