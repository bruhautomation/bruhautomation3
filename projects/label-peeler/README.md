# Label Peeler

A printed holder that fixes a single-edge razor blade at a shallow, constant
angle so a label comes off a container in one piece, and your thumb stays behind
the edge. Heat is optional and it is what turns a twenty-minute job into a
two-minute one.

**Build guide:** [bruhautomation.com/projects/label-peeler](https://bruhautomation.com/projects/label-peeler/)

## Hardware

- 3D-printed blade holder — PETG, not PLA, if you are going anywhere near a heat
  gun with it
- Single-edge steel razor blades — for glass only
- Plastic razor blades — for plastic, painted and coated containers
- M3 screw and a heat-set insert to clamp the blade, so it can be changed
- Heat gun with a low setting (optional; a hair dryer will do a paper label)
- 99% isopropyl alcohol and a microfibre cloth for the adhesive left behind

## Notes

Steel on glass, plastic on everything else — a steel blade scratches HDPE, PET,
paint and anodising, and on a curved surface it skates. Never hold a heat gun
still on cold glass, and never point one at a plastic bottle: PET draws in and
goes oval well below the temperature that softens the adhesive.
