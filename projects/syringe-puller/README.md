# 10mL Syringe Puller

A printed bench jig for drawing a 10 mL syringe plunger evenly and one-handed.
The barrel is held by its finger flange, the plunger is taken back against a
stop, and the pull is the same every time — no stiction lurch at the start, no
overshoot at the end. For sample draws, priming a line, and pulling a fixed
volume through a microfluidic chip.

**Build guide:** [bruhautomation.com/projects/syringe-puller](https://bruhautomation.com/projects/syringe-puller/)

## Hardware

- 10 mL **Luer-lock** syringes — a slip tip walks off under a steady pull. The
  jig is dimensioned around the barrel diameter and the finger flange, and both
  vary by brand, so measure the ones your lab actually stocks
- PETG for the printed parts, with a TPU pad where the flange lands. PETG
  because a jig that lives under load creeps if it is PLA, and because this gets
  wiped with 70% alcohol every time it is used
- M3 brass heat-set inserts and **stainless** M3 hardware — black-zinc screws
  bloom rust after a few months of alcohol wipes
- Four self-adhesive bumpers under the base, or it skates on a wiped bench

Not autoclavable, and not meant to be. The syringe is the sterile part.
