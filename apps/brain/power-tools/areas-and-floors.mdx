Ask brAIn to build and maintain the rooms and floors of your home, and to put every device and entity in the right one.

## Ask brAIn

```text
Move every hallway light to the Hallway area.
Create a Guest Room area on the upstairs floor, with "spare room" as a voice alias.
Put the master bedroom and the kids' room on the upstairs floor.
The basement is level -1, not 0. Fix it.
Make the living room also answer to "lounge" and "front room".
Put the under-cabinet light in the kitchen, even though its hub lives elsewhere.
```

brAIn looks up the ids, checks every one, makes the change, and tells you what moved.

## What brAIn can do

Every service is admin-only. Call one from **Developer tools → Actions**, a script or an automation, or ask brAIn. \* marks a required field, and `[]` marks a field that takes one value or a list.

### Areas

| Service | What it does | Fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `brain.create_area` | Create an area. Returns its `area_id`. | `name`\*, `icon`, `aliases[]`, `floor_id` |
| `brain.rename_area` | Rename an area. | `area_id`\*, `name`\* |
| `brain.set_area_aliases` | Replace the alternative names voice assistants listen for. Send the full new list. | `area_id`\*, `aliases[]`\* |
| `brain.set_area_icon` | Set the area's icon. Leave `icon` out to clear it. | `area_id`\*, `icon` |
| `brain.delete_area` | Delete an area. Its devices and entities are left unassigned. `dry_run: true` lists what would lose its area. | `area_id`\*, `dry_run` |
| `brain.add_device_to_area` | Move devices into an area. | `area_id`\*, `device_id[]`\* |
| `brain.remove_device_from_area` | Take devices out of whatever area they are in. | `device_id[]`\* |
| `brain.add_entity_to_area` | Move entities into an area. This overrides the area of the device they belong to. | `area_id`\*, `entity_id[]`\* |
| `brain.remove_entity_from_area` | Take entities out of whatever area they are in. | `entity_id[]`\* |

### Floors

| Service | What it does | Fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `brain.create_floor` | Create a floor. Returns its `floor_id`. `level` is the storey number: 0 for the ground floor. | `name`\*, `icon`, `level`, `aliases[]` |
| `brain.rename_floor` | Rename a floor. | `floor_id`\*, `name`\* |
| `brain.update_floor` | Change a floor's icon, level or aliases. Fields you leave out stay as they are. Use a negative `level` for a basement. | `floor_id`\*, `icon`, `level`, `aliases[]` |
| `brain.delete_floor` | Delete a floor. Its areas are left without a floor. `dry_run: true` lists them. | `floor_id`\*, `dry_run` |
| `brain.add_area_to_floor` | Put areas on a floor. | `floor_id`\*, `area_id[]`\* |
| `brain.remove_area_from_floor` | Take areas off whatever floor they are on. | `area_id[]`\* |

## Good to know

- A device gives its area to all of its entities. Assign an **entity** to an area to override that, for example when one hub owns gadgets in different rooms.
- Aliases are what voice assistants use to resolve "turn off the lights upstairs" or "the lounge".
- Find ids by asking brAIn, or in the pickers under **Developer tools → Actions**.

## Examples

```yaml title="Create a room on a floor"
action: brain.create_area
data:
  name: Guest Room
  icon: mdi:bed
  aliases: ["spare room"]
  floor_id: upstairs
response_variable: area   # area.area_id
```

```yaml title="Move a device, and one entity that lives elsewhere"
- action: brain.add_device_to_area
  data:
    area_id: office
    device_id:
      - a1b2c3d4e5f6
- action: brain.add_entity_to_area
  data:
    area_id: kitchen
    entity_id:
      - light.under_cabinet
```

```yaml title="Create a floor and fill it"
- action: brain.create_floor
  data:
    name: Upstairs
    level: 1
    icon: mdi:home-floor-2
- action: brain.add_area_to_floor
  data:
    floor_id: upstairs
    area_id:
      - master_bedroom
      - kids_room
```

```yaml title="See what deleting an area would unassign"
action: brain.delete_area
data:
  area_id: guest_room
  dry_run: true
response_variable: preview
```
