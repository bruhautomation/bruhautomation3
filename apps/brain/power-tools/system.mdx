These Power Tools reach the parts of Home Assistant that sit furthest behind the Settings
screens. Like every Power Tool they are **admin-only**, a voice agent at the *Voice assistant*
level cannot use them, and most **preview by default**: set `dry_run: false` to make the change.
Ask in brAIn's chat (*"prune the backups older than a month but keep the newest three"*) or call
them from an automation.

## Backups

| Service | What it does | Fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `brain.list_backups` | Every backup Home Assistant knows, newest first: name, date, size, where it is kept, and whether it was automatic, partial or password-protected. Home Assistant 2025.1 or later. | — |
| `brain.create_backup` | Make a backup now: full, or partial on Home Assistant OS and Supervised. Waits until it is made. | `partial`, `name`, `password`, `homeassistant`, `addons`, `folders` |
| `brain.delete_backups` | Delete the backups a filter picks (by id, age, name or kind), keeping the newest few if asked. A call with no filter, or one that would leave no backup at all, is refused. | `backup_id`, `older_than_days`, `name_contains`, `kind`, `keep_newest`, `dry_run` |

```yaml
action: brain.delete_backups
data:
  older_than_days: 30
  keep_newest: 3
  dry_run: false
```

## Statistics and the energy dashboard

Fix the energy dashboard's history the way the Statistics screen does. Each shows what it would
change first.

| Service | What it does | Fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `brain.adjust_statistic_sum` | Add an amount to a meter's long-term sum from one hour onward, to fix a wrong reading. | `statistic_id`, `start_time`, `adjustment`, `unit`, `dry_run` |
| `brain.heal_statistic_spikes` | Take out the hours where a meter's sum jumped absurdly: a counter glitch near 2^30, a sum that fell, or a change over the bound you give. | `statistic_id`, `max_hourly_change`, `days`, `dry_run` |
| `brain.clear_orphaned_statistics` | Delete the statistics the recorder keeps for entities that no longer exist. External statistics are never touched. | `statistic_id`, `dry_run` |
| `brain.get_energy_prefs` | The energy dashboard's sources (grid, solar, battery, gas, water) and individual devices. | — |
| `brain.save_energy_prefs` | Replace its sources and/or devices. Every statistic named must be one the recorder keeps; a list given replaces the whole list. | `energy_sources`, `device_consumption`, `device_consumption_water`, `dry_run` |

## Z-Wave, Thread and Home Assistant's addresses

| Service | What it does | Fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `brain.zwave_remove_failed_node` | Remove a Z-Wave node the controller has marked dead, as the device page's *Remove failed device* does. A node that is not dead is refused. | `device_id` or `entity_id`, `dry_run` |
| `brain.zwave_rebuild_node_routes` | Ask the controller to rebuild one node's routes, and say whether it worked. | `device_id` or `entity_id` |
| `brain.list_thread_datasets` | The Thread networks Home Assistant knows, with name, channel and which is preferred. The network key is never returned. | — |
| `brain.set_preferred_thread_dataset` | Make one the preferred network new Thread devices join. | `dataset_id` |
| `brain.set_core_urls` | Set the internal and/or external address Home Assistant is reached at. Empty clears it. | `internal_url`, `external_url`, `dry_run` |

## Categories

The categories on the automation, script, scene and helper lists.

| Service | What it does | Fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `brain.create_category` | Make a category. Returns its `category_id`. | `scope`, `name`, `icon` |
| `brain.update_category` | Rename it or change its icon. | `scope`, `category_id`, `name`, `icon` |
| `brain.delete_category` | Delete it. What was in it is left uncategorised, and listed. | `scope`, `category_id`, `dry_run` |
| `brain.set_entity_category` | Put automations, scripts, scenes or helpers in a category, or take them out by leaving it empty. | `entity_id`, `scope`, `category_id` |

## Integrations, groups, users and dashboards

| Service | What it does | Fields |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `brain.set_integration_options` | Change an integration's options through its own **Configure** form. Only the fields named change; a form of more than one step, or a menu, is refused with what it asked for. | `config_entry_id`, `options`, `dry_run` |
| `brain.set_group_members` | Set, add to or take from the members of a group helper made in the UI. Members must be the group's kind. Returns the members before and after. | `entity_id`, `entities`, `add`, `remove`, `dry_run` |
| `brain.list_users` | Every user with their groups, whether they are active, local-only or a system user, and when each of their sign-ins was last used, so an unused account can be shown to be unused. | `include_system`, `unused_for_days` |
| `brain.set_user_group` | Make a user an administrator, an ordinary user or read-only. The owner, system users and the last administrator are refused. | `user_id`, `group`, `dry_run` |
| `brain.update_dashboard_info` | A storage dashboard's own settings: sidebar title and icon, whether it shows in the sidebar, whether only admins can see it. | `url_path`, `title`, `icon`, `show_in_sidebar`, `require_admin`, `dry_run` |
