**Home → Ideas** is a page of insight cards brAIn thinks your house is missing. Each one
says what brAIn found that made it think so, and what the card would answer every time it
runs. Take one and it becomes an ordinary card on [Insights](/brain/insights/); say no and
it's never suggested again.

![The Ideas tab, headed "Ideas for new cards", with a Suggest ideas button and three ideas: "Heat pump against everything else", "Garage freezer, on its own" and "Which rooms the dehumidifier is fighting", each with a "Why this house" and a "What it would answer" box and the buttons Add this card and Not for this house](./images/panel-ideas.webp)

## Why a separate page

Your first cards are chosen once, when brAIn [first studies your house](/brain/insights/#where-cards-come-from).
A house that has since grown a heat pump, a solar array and four months of history would
otherwise go on generating whatever was picked in its first twenty minutes.

Ideas are kept apart from Insights on purpose. **An idea costs nothing until you take it**;
a card costs a Claude run every time it refreshes. That split is what lets the page be
generous.

## Reading an idea

Every idea carries three things:

| | Example |
| --- | --- |
| **The card's name** | *Garage freezer, on its own* |
| **Why this house** — what brAIn actually found | *It has drifted six degrees over a month while the kitchen one has not moved.* |
| **What it would answer**, every run | *Is the garage freezer still holding its temperature?* |

If the *why* could have been written about any house, it isn't a good idea — and brAIn is
told so.

## Taking or refusing one

- **✓ Add this card** creates the card on Insights and generates it. From then on it's a
  card like any other: [Refine](/brain/insights/#refine-change-a-card-by-saying-whats-different)
  it, share it, give it a schedule.
- **✕ Not for this house** removes the idea and remembers that you said no. It won't be
  suggested again in any wording.

## When new ideas appear

- **Press ✨ Suggest ideas** for a fresh look now. It runs even if automatic work is paused
  or the usage budget is spent — anything you press by hand always runs.
- **Once a week** brAIn tops the page up on its own, if automatic insights are on and
  there's budget to spare. It only ever *adds*: it never clears ideas you haven't answered.

brAIn never suggests a card you already have — however that card came to exist — or one you
already answered.

An empty page always says which of three things is true: nobody has asked yet, the last look
failed (and why), or brAIn looked and had nothing to add. Only the middle one needs anything
from you.
