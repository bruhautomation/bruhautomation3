## What it is

brAIn is a Home Assistant add-on that runs **Claude Code** inside your house, with a suite
of tools built for Home Assistant and a memory that grows the longer it lives there.

It sees all of Home Assistant: every entity, device, area, floor, dashboard, helper and
automation. It can change any of it. It also watches the house on its own. It notices
when something goes wrong, works out whether it matters before it bothers you, explains
what happened and why, and keeps a plain-language record of how your home works.

Everything comes in through **one sidebar entry and one Claude login**. Your Claude **Pro**
or **Max** subscription pays for it, or you can use an API key. There is no brAIn
subscription.

## The panel

brAIn's sidebar entry opens on up to four tabs, with ⚙ Settings in the top bar. On a phone
the tabs move to a bar along the bottom.

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Morph UI** (Home) | The [Morph dashboard](/brain/morph/), brAIn's face: a dashboard that knows what matters right now and says why, which brAIn designs and edits with you. Shown while **Morph dashboard** is on in [⚙ › Features](/brain/features/). Every tab's name and icon can be changed in ⚙ › Dashboard. |
| **Insights** | **Cards**: your [insight cards](/brain/insights/), each an answer with the numbers and a chart. **Needs you**: [every decision waiting on you](/brain/needs-you/) as one kind of card, with **To Do** under it. **History**: what you've already answered, each with **Restore**. |
| **Ask** | [Claude Code](/brain/terminal/) as a chat that reflows to a phone, or as a real terminal. Several conversations stay open at once. |
| **Memory** | **Knowledge**: [every fact brAIn holds](/brain/memory/), by room and device, each deletable. **Timeline**: [what the house is doing now and what caused each change](/brain/activity/). **House book**: [how this house works](/brain/upkeep/#the-house-book), chapter by chapter. |

![The brAIn panel's Insights tab: Cards, Needs you and History along the top, and insight cards such as "Why did the hallway light come on at 3 am?" with the automation trace that explains it, and "Downstairs dries 15 min before it cools" with humidity and temperature charts](./images/panel-insights.webp)

⚙ holds Features, Account, Model & usage, Permissions, Notifications, Cameras & calendars,
Personality, Instructions, Diagnostics, Developer and the built-in Guide. The full
documentation ships inside the add-on and works with no internet.

## Use cases

These are things brAIn does today, on an ordinary house.

### A leak at 3 am

The washing machine's leak sensor trips at 03:12. No model is asked first: brAIn files a
**critical** card naming the sensor and the time and sends it to your phone straight away,
through quiet hours. Nobody answers, so it asks again an hour later, then four hours later,
then twelve. If the sensor dries out the card says so and the reminders stop, but the card
stays until you answer it, because the water still came from somewhere. If you ticked the
utility room's camera, the investigation afterwards may look through it to confirm. None of
this needs a Claude sign-in, a usage budget or automatic runs to be on.
[Notifications →](/brain/notifications/)

### "Why did the hallway light come on at 3 am?"

Ask it in **Ask**, or in the *Ask or tell the house* line on the Morph dashboard. brAIn reads
the logbook and the automation's trace and answers in words: the night-bathroom automation
fired on landing motion and never set a brightness, so the light came back at its last
evening value. Say *"fix it"* and it edits the automation, validates the YAML, reloads, and
tells you that `brain undo` puts the old file back. **Save as report** turns the answer into
an insight card.

![The Ask tab in chat mode: the question "The hallway light came on at 3am again. Find out why and fix it.", a collapsed "Worked through 6 steps", and brAIn's answer explaining the missing brightness and the fix it made](./images/panel-chat.webp)

### The freezer door nobody closed

At the hour your house usually settles, brAIn checks every door, window, lock, cover and
garage against how much of that hour each one is normally open, measured from a month of
your own history. The garage freezer's door is open at 23:30 and it never is. You get one
row before bed, not one per door. The slow failure is covered too: a freezer drifting a few
degrees warmer over weeks never looks unusual on any single day, so brAIn also fits a trend
through the month and tells you when a reading has been walking one way.
[Measurements →](/brain/measurements/)

### A dead battery, handled from the lock screen

The back door sensor has reported nothing since Tuesday. A house check files it, the
Resident looks at its history before you see it, and the card says what it found. On your
phone the notification carries **Add to To Do**, **Snooze** and **Ignore**, plus a **Reply**
box. Type *"I already replaced it"* and it is marked fixed. Type *"why does this matter?"*
and brAIn answers in the next notification. The same card is in Home Assistant's Repairs.
[Needs you →](/brain/needs-you/)

### Asking out loud

*"Is anyone still in the garage?"*, *"Turn the lounge down for a film"*, *"Print three
freezer labels for bolognese"*, *"Remember that we call the office lamp the beacon."*
brAIn answers Assist from a pool of pre-warmed workers with your memory and an area map
already loaded. The default agent reaches only what you expose to Assist.
[Voice →](/brain/voice/)

### Automations that ask brAIn

An automation can hand brAIn a question and get the answer back as data:

```yaml
action: brain.ask
data:
  question: Is anything in the house drawing more than usual right now?
  schema:
    type: object
    properties:
      unusual: {type: boolean}
      why: {type: string}
    required: [unusual, why]
response_variable: answer
# answer.data.unusual is a boolean you can put in a condition
```

It is read-only unless you say otherwise. Home Assistant's own **Suggest with AI** buttons
and `ai_task.generate_data` can be answered by brAIn too.
[Automations →](/brain/automations/)

### A rule in one sentence

*"Whenever the front door opens after dark, turn the hall light on."* brAIn drafts the
automation, replays it over the last month of your recorder, and offers it with the case
in one line: it would have fired nine times, and you'd already done the same yourself on
four. Try it for a week first if you like. [Rules & one-offs →](/brain/intents/)

### A house that grew by accident

*"Every light in the basement should be in a Basement area on a Lower Floor, and rename the
Sonoff switches to what they actually control."* brAIn does the whole sweep through Home
Assistant's own registries: areas, floors, labels, names, aliases, what a device shows as.
[Power Tools →](/brain/power-tools/)

## It learns, and you can see what it learned

brAIn's memory is not a black box. **Memory → Knowledge** lists every fact it holds, by room
and device, each with who taught it and a bin beside it. When it believes something it
cannot confirm, it asks one yes/no question on Needs you. Every **Ignore** with a reason
becomes a correction, so the same non-problem is never raised at you twice in new words.

![Memory → Knowledge: a list of rooms and devices on the left, and facts such as "The fridge sits at 3–4 °C; above 6 °C for an hour means the door was left open" with a bin beside each, and a Teach box under them](./images/panel-memory.webp)

Four switches in [⚙ › Features](/brain/features/) decide how far it goes: the **Morph
dashboard**, **Personality** (a name and voice you choose, everywhere), the **Learning
loop** (brAIn improving itself on this house, with nothing leaving it) and the
**Development loop** (reporting its own faults, redacted, to a private GitHub repository
you own). [Learning →](/brain/learning/)

## Where your data goes

- **Everything runs in your Home Assistant.** The add-on, its memory, its measurements and
  its stores live on your machine, under `/config/.brain/` and the add-on's own storage.
- **Claude runs go to Anthropic**, under your own account, as with any use of Claude Code:
  the prompt, and what brAIn read from your house to answer it. Many things cost no Claude
  run at all: the house checks, the measurements, safety alerts and overnight self-healing.
- **The development loop is the only thing that sends anything elsewhere.** It is off by
  default, it sends to a **private** GitHub repository you own, and names, rooms and entity
  ids are replaced with aliases first. [Development loop →](/brain/development-loop/)
- **Your Claude credential is kept out of Home Assistant backups.** Sharing the login with
  the other BRUH apps is a switch, off by default, and the panel says what it costs.
- **Cameras are off until you tick one**, and brAIn looks through a ticked camera on its own
  at most a dozen times a day. [Permissions & Safety →](/brain/safety/#cameras)

## What you need

- **Home Assistant OS** or **Supervised**: add-ons need the Supervisor. Home Assistant
  2023.6 or later; some features need newer releases (the To-do list 2023.11, AI Task 2025.7).
- A **64-bit host**: `amd64` or `aarch64`. A Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 is fine.
- About **1 GB of free memory** beyond Home Assistant's own.
- An **Anthropic account**. A Claude **Pro** or **Max** subscription is the most economical,
  and is what the usage-limit sensors read. An API key works too.

<Aside type="caution" title="Back up Home Assistant first, somewhere that isn't this machine">
brAIn edits your real configuration: automations, dashboards, helpers, entities. It
snapshots files before it changes them and `brain undo` puts them back, but that is not a
backup you can restore from. **Settings → System → Backups**, then copy it off the device.
</Aside>

<Aside type="note" title="Experimental">
brAIn is actively developed and changes often, above all the Morph dashboard. Keep your own
dashboards and automations working beside it.
</Aside>

**[Quick Start →](/brain/quickstart/)**: add the repository, install (a prebuilt image, not a
local build), restart Home Assistant once, sign in, and press **Start learning**. About five
minutes, plus a few minutes of study in the background.
