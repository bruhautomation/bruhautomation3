Morph notices how the dashboard is used and suggests changes that fit. You answer each
suggestion, and the lessons it keeps shape every later change.

<Aside type="tip" title="Nothing leaves your house">
The usage log stays in your house and the loop runs on your own brAIn. It is part of brAIn's
**Learning loop** switch in ⚙ › Features. With the switch off, nothing here runs on its own.
</Aside>

## How it works

| Step | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Morph logs use** | What you open, toggle and slide, when, and what you ask brAIn for. |
| **2. A learning pass runs** | **Daily** by default (set it in ⚙ › Dashboard). Morph asks brAIn how the dashboard could fit that use better. The pass is read-only: it can look at the house and change nothing. |
| **3. You get suggestions** | Filed as brAIn's cases, where you answer everything else. |
| **4. Lessons are revised** | Kept in `/config/morph/LEARNED.md`. Every Tell and every pass reads them. |

The loop is about the dashboard only. It suggests no automations or renames. Learning the
house is [brAIn's own job](/brain/learning/).

A pass that fails (brAIn stopped, signed out, out of time) is tried again on the next
schedule. **Learn now** in ⚙ › Dashboard runs one straight away.

## Answer a suggestion

Answer in words or with the buttons.

| Answer | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Yes** | brAIn makes the change as an ordinary [Tell](/brain/morph/tell-brain/), with Keep and Undo. |
| **Dismiss** | Hides it for now. |
| **No** (with a reason, if you like) | It is never suggested again. |

- A suggestion never removes a card that holds a control (a light, a switch, a lock), even
  one rarely tapped.
- A No to a reorder is remembered for every section that move would change, however it is
  worded next time.

Two other kinds of suggestion:

| Suggestion | Answers |
| --- | --- |
| **Fix the card code …**, when a file in `/config/morph/cards/` will not load | Yes fixes it with a Tell. |
| A change to Morph's own code | **Do it** (brAIn changes your copy of Morph, then asks whether to open an issue), **Send as issue** (only report it), or **No**. |

Nothing is sent until you press **Send** or **Open an issue**, and only while brAIn's
**Development loop** switch is on.

## Read and edit the lessons

Edit the lessons in **⚙ › Dashboard** or in `/config/morph/LEARNED.md`. Each line ends with
who wrote it:

```markdown
- Keep the Kitchen first on the ground floor; it is the room used most at breakfast. (learned)
- Never put a camera on the wall tablet's first screen. (you)
- The Office thermostat card shows the humidity under the temperature. (tell)
```

| Mark | Written by |
| --- | --- |
| **(you)** | You. |
| **(tell)** | brAIn, during a Tell. |
| **(learned)** | A learning pass. |

- A line you write, or any line with no mark, is never reworded or dropped by Morph.
- A pass adds a lesson only with two pieces of evidence or a reason you typed.
- A reason you give for a No is read as evidence. It is not written down as a rule.

## Check the scorecard

**⚙ › Dashboard** shows how the loop is doing: how many suggestions were made, done and
declined, and how many of brAIn's changes were kept or undone.

## Examples

- **The evening office.** Alex opens the Office every evening and adjusts the desk lamp, but
  the Office is the last room on the page. A pass suggests moving it up. Alex says yes, and
  brAIn makes the move as a Tell.
- **A no with a reason.** A pass suggests dropping the Yard's weather card because nobody
  opens it. Sam answers No: *"I glance at it every morning, I just don't tap it."* The
  suggestion never comes back.
- **Your own rule.** You add *"Lights are always one row, never two."* to `LEARNED.md`. It
  has no mark, so Morph never rewrites it, and every Tell follows it.
