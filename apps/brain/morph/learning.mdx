Morph gets better at being *your* dashboard the more it is used. It keeps a log of what you use,
and on a schedule asks brAIn how the dashboard could fit that better. What comes back are
**suggestions** you answer, and **lessons** that every later change follows.

<Aside type="tip" title="Nothing leaves your house">
The usage log never leaves your house, and the learning loop runs on your own brAIn. It is part
of brAIn's **Learning loop** switch in ⚙ › Features: off, nothing here runs on its own, and brAIn
still does what you ask.
</Aside>

## What Morph notices

Morph keeps a log of what you use: what you open, toggle and slide, when, and what you ask brAIn
for.

## The learning pass

On a schedule — **daily by default**, set in ⚙ › Dashboard — Morph asks brAIn how the
**dashboard** could be better for how it is actually used. The pass is **read-only**: it can look
at the house but change nothing.

This loop is about the dashboard only. Learning the house is brAIn's own job, so a pass suggests
no automations or renames, and its lessons are rules about how you want your dashboard.

A pass that fails — brAIn stopped, signed out, out of time — is tried again on the next schedule;
it does not count as having read what you did. **Learn now** in ⚙ › Dashboard runs one straight
away.

## What comes back

### Suggestions

Filed as brAIn's cases, so you answer them where you answer everything else. Answer in words or
with the buttons:

- **Yes** hands the suggestion to brAIn as an ordinary [Tell](/brain/morph/tell-brain/) — with
  Keep and Undo like any other.
- **Dismiss** hides it for now.
- **No** (with a reason, if you like) means it is **never suggested again**.

Suggestions never remove a card that holds a control (a light, a switch, a lock), even one rarely
tapped, and a No to a reorder is remembered for every section that move would change, however it
is worded next time.

### Lessons

Revised each pass in `/config/morph/LEARNED.md`. **Every Tell and every pass reads them.** Each
line ends with who wrote it:

| Mark | Written by |
| --- | --- |
| **(you)** | You. |
| **(tell)** | brAIn, during a Tell. |
| **(learned)** | A learning pass. |

**A line you write — or any line with no mark — is never reworded or dropped by Morph.** A pass
adds a lesson only with two pieces of evidence or a reason you typed; a reason you give for No is
read as evidence, not written down as a rule. Edit the lessons in **⚙ › Dashboard** or in the file.

A lesson file might read:

```markdown
- Keep the Kitchen first on the ground floor; it is the room used most at breakfast. (learned)
- Never put a camera on the wall tablet's first screen. (you)
- The Office thermostat card shows the humidity under the temperature. (tell)
```

### A broken card of your own

When a file in `/config/morph/cards/` will not load, Morph offers **Fix the card code …** as a
suggestion, since that code is this house's and a Tell fixes it.

### A change to Morph itself

Something the pass thinks Morph's own code should do differently comes with three answers:

- **Do it** — brAIn changes your copy of Morph, then asks whether to open an issue.
- **Send as issue** — only report it.
- **No**.

Nothing is sent until you press **Send** or **Open an issue**, and only while brAIn's
**Development loop** switch is on.

## The scorecard

**⚙ › Dashboard** shows how the loop is doing: how many suggestions were made, done and declined,
and how many of brAIn's changes were kept or undone.

## Use cases

**The evening office.** Every evening Alex opens the Office and adjusts the desk lamp, but the
Office is the last room on the page. After enough evenings, the pass suggests moving it up. Alex
says yes; brAIn makes the move as a Tell.

**"No, and here's why."** The pass suggests dropping the Yard's weather card because nobody opens
it. Sam answers No: *"I glance at it every morning, I just don't tap it"*. That reason becomes evidence,
and the suggestion never comes back.

**Your own rule, kept.** You add a line to `LEARNED.md`: *"Lights are always one row, never
two."* It has no mark, so Morph never rewrites or drops it — and every Tell from then on follows
it.
