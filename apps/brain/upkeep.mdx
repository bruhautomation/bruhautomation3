These jobs look after Home Assistant itself: names nobody tidied, devices in no room,
updates you are not sure about, the error log nobody reads, and the knowledge of how the
house works that lives in one person's head.

| You want to | brAIn does | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Names, rooms and aliases

brAIn reads a sample of the names *you* chose first, so its suggestions follow your
house's style. They arrive as one **Tidy-up** card marked *Change ready*.

| Kind | What it fixes |
| --- | --- |
| **Rename** | Names that are still the hardware's (`0x00158d0001a2b3c4`, `Shelly Plus 1PM 8A4C`), which you cannot find in a picker or say to Assist. |
| **Room** | Entities and devices in no area, which "turn off the kitchen" and every area card cannot see. |
| **Alias** | Spoken aliases for what people actually say. |

<Steps>

1. Open the card's **Details**. Every change is a ticked row.
2. Untick what you disagree with. Press **Change** on a row to give a different name, or to
   pick a room from your own list.
3. Press **Apply**.

</Steps>

- **A room move says what it changes.** If moving a device changes what an automation
  reaches, the card says so and Details names the automation.
- **Apply re-reads every field** from Home Assistant before writing and records only what
  Home Assistant accepted.
- **Undo** is in **History → Done** for 30 days. It never overwrites a rename you made
  since.
- brAIn never suggests a change to a protected entity, a room that does not exist, or a
  name another entity already answers to.

A *named after its hardware* or *no room* problem on Needs you starts this from its
**Go ahead** press.

## Updates waiting

Each pending update brAIn has read the release notes for is a card on Needs you.

| Verdict | What it means | First press |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Safe tonight** | Nothing in the notes reaches what your house runs. | **Apply** installs it, with *What could go wrong* on the card. Home Assistant backs up first where the update offers it. |
| **Wait** | Something in the notes affects you. The card names the edit to make first. | **Add to To Do** |
| **Can't tell** | brAIn could not quote its evidence, or could not fetch the notes. The card says what it could not reach. | **Add to To Do**, with **Check again** under **⋯** |

A *safe* or *wait* has to quote two lines, shown under **Details**: one from the release
notes, and one from your configuration or from the device itself. Both are checked word
for word against what was actually read. An answer that cannot quote both is *can't tell*.

<Aside type="caution" title="What it never does">
- **It never installs an update unattended.** Installing is your press on **Apply**, offered
  only on an update checked as safe for exactly the version waiting. An update cannot be
  undone from the card.
- **It never reads `secrets.yaml`.** Any configuration line whose key looks like a
  credential is blanked, and Home Assistant's storage and the recorder database are
  skipped.
- **Release notes are data, never instructions.** The run that reads them holds reading
  tools only.
</Aside>

## Overnight health check

Once a night, between **03:00 and 06:00**, and whenever you press **Run** beside *Overnight health
check* in ⚙ → Diagnostics, brAIn reads:

- Home Assistant's error log;
- the Zigbee mesh: routers, weak links, devices not seen;
- Z-Wave node statistics and status;
- automations that switch a smart plug feeding a Zigbee router.

It names **at most five root causes**, as causes, not symptoms:

> *The hallway plug that the upstairs Zigbee sensors route through keeps being switched off
> by the bedtime automation.*

- **Every cause cites the records it rests on**: a log line, a Zigbee device, a Z-Wave node,
  an automation. A cause that cites nothing real is dropped.
- **Passing errors are held back.** A burst around a restart that then stopped is not
  filed. An integration is a cause only when it is still broken.
- **A cause is one card, not one a night.** Reworded tomorrow, it is the same finding. An
  error you explained with **Ignore** stays explained.
- **A source that could not be read clears nothing.** *I could not look* is not *it went
  away*.

Causes reach Needs you through the same [first look](/brain/checks/) as every finding. The
scheduled run needs a Claude sign-in, **Automatic insights** on and usage budget left. A
quiet night costs nothing.

## The house book

**Memory → House book** is how this house works, written for a partner, a sitter or the
person buying the house. brAIn writes it from your automations, scripts, scenes and what it
has learned.

![Memory → House book: Add info and a ⋯ menu at the top, the line "Every sentence names what it came from; codes and passwords are left out", a search box, a room picker and chapter pills, then two chapters. "If an alarm goes off" says the house closes the mains water valve and texts Ben, cited to "Leak: close the water" and "Mains valve". "Heating and cooling" is cited to "Warm before wake". Each has Add to this section, and unwritten chapters are listed under "Not written yet"](./images/panel2-housebook.webp)

Chapters: what the house does on its own, lights and scenes, heating and cooling, locks and
security, what to do when an alarm goes off, shutoffs and where things are, appliances,
media and voice, and anything else worth knowing.

| To | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Write the first book | Press **Add info** and leave the box blank. |
| Document something | **Add info**, then type it: *the stopcock is under the kitchen sink*, *explain the bedtime routine*. brAIn writes it in, cited to you. |
| Fill in one chapter | **Add to this section** under it. Blank fills the chapter from the house. |
| Put your exact words in | **Add as written**. No Claude run. |
| Correct or remove a line | The line's **⋯ → Edit this line** or **Delete this line**. |
| Find something | Search, the room picker and the chapter pills. |

- **Every sentence names what it came from.** A sentence brAIn cannot cite is left out.
- **Codes and passwords are left out, always.**
- **Your words survive.** A line you wrote or corrected is kept when brAIn rewrites the
  book.
- **Gaps become questions** on Needs you, such as *where is the water shutoff?* brAIn looks
  in Home Assistant first and says what it found. Your answer goes into memory in your
  words.
- **It keeps itself current.** After the first book, brAIn rewrites it weekly, and only when
  something it reads has changed.

### Share it, and take it back

**⋯ → Share a copy** gives you a private link to a read-only copy under your Home
Assistant's `/local` folder. The copy is redacted, has no script, asks search engines to
keep away, and stays current.

**Stop sharing** deletes the page **and changes the link**, so a link somebody kept stops
working. The book's link has its own secret, separate from your shared insight cards.

## Who can reach the house

Four [house checks](/brain/checks/#rules-worth-knowing-about) look at access and file
ordinary cards:

| Check | What it reports |
| --- | --- |
| `sec.lock_cloud_voice` | A lock or alarm panel exposed to Alexa or Google through Home Assistant Cloud. |
| `sec.addon_unprotected` | An app whose protection mode has been switched off. |
| `sec.login_bans` | Recent failed logins that got an address banned. |
| `sec.brain_posture` | A terminal session still acting without asking after *Let brAIn act without asking* was turned **off**. Turning it on is your choice and files nothing. |

A check that could not look says nothing. **Ignore** with a reason stops it for that lock
or app.

Once a week brAIn also writes **one sentence** about who and what can reach the house, from
what the last checks read. It is in ⚙ → Diagnostics under *Who can reach the house*, with
when it was written, and **Run** writes it on demand. It follows the same rules as every
scheduled run.
