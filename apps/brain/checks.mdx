brAIn runs 68 checks over your house every six hours and files what is broken on
**Needs you**. A pass takes seconds, uses no Claude run, and clears each row when its
problem goes away.

![Needs you: three cards that checks filed. "Bedroom is losing heat faster than it can" from a climate check, "Garage freezer temperature has been drifting up for weeks" from a forecast, and "Nursery is still warming up when this house gets up", each with its readings and a Fix line](./images/think-needs-you.webp)

Before you see a card, [the Resident](/brain/resident/) looks into it and either rewrites
it with what it found or sets it aside with a reason.

## Run the checks

| How | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Automatically | Every 6 hours (`checks_interval_hours`, 0 to 168), plus one pass around your measured bedtime |
| `brain check` | Runs every check now and prints what changed |
| `brain check list` | Lists every check and when it last ran |
| `brain check --json` | Prints the raw result |
| `POST /api/checks/run` | Runs a pass from a script |

## Answer a card

- **Fix the problem** and do nothing else. The next pass removes the row. A row that clears
  on its own leaves no line in memory, and the check files it again if it comes back.
- **Ignore** it with a reason. brAIn writes that into memory and does not raise the same
  thing again. **Ignore all like this** and **Stop raising this about…** widen the answer.
- **Stop watching…** on the Ignore box means no check looks at that device again. It is not
  offered for safety, security or protected things.

The full set of buttons is on [Needs you](/brain/needs-you/).

## What gets checked

Every check, by family. The id is what `brain check list` prints.

| Check | What it catches |
| --- | --- |
| `safety.tripped` | A leak, smoke, CO or gas detector reading tripped right now |

## Rules worth knowing about

- **A safety automation is never offered for deletion.** `auto.never_fired` leaves alone any
  automation watching a smoke, gas, moisture, carbon monoxide or safety sensor, any alarm
  panel, and anything brAIn wrote itself, including the
  [emergency playbooks](/brain/playbooks/).
- **A battery you charge is not a battery to replace.** Phones and tablets, robot vacuums
  and mowers, electric cars, home batteries, UPSs and anything that reports charging are
  left out of the battery checks or told to charge. **Ignore** on a battery card stops that
  battery being reported again.
- **A night-only rule is not broken.** `auto.condition_never_passes` waits until an
  automation's actions have not run for two weeks.
- **Automations built in the new editor are understood.** Triggers such as "When a light
  turns on" are read by the trigger checks and can be [replayed](/brain/replay/).
- **Access checks respect your choices.** **Ignore** with a reason stops a check for that
  lock or app. Turning **Let brAIn act without asking** on files nothing.
  `sec.brain_posture` is only for a terminal session still acting after you turned it off.
  Once a week brAIn also writes one sentence about who and what can reach the house, shown
  on [Upkeep](/brain/upkeep/).
- **One device, one card.** Checks that would name the same thing stand down for each
  other, and network drops are grouped.

## When a check cannot look

A check that could not read its data reports nothing and clears nothing. This keeps "I
could not look" apart from "it went away".

- **⚙ → Diagnostics** names the checks that could not run and why.
- When Home Assistant will not answer about recent runs, the three trace checks say they
  could not look.
- When recorder history has a hole, checks that read history stand down for that pass and
  `sys.history_incomplete` says why.
- A check about something your house does not have (no HACS, no Matter, no Energy
  configuration) is listed as *not set up here*.

## When you hear about them

Each check declares how urgent it is. Severity and urgency together decide how a finding
reaches your phone:

| Tier | Which findings | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Escalate** | `critical` and urgent: a leak, a freeze, a hub that stopped answering | Sent straight away through quiet hours, then repeated up to three times until somebody answers |
| **Notify** | At or above `findings_notify_min_severity` (default `critical`) | Sent once. Held through quiet hours unless the check cannot wait |
| **Quiet** | Everything else | No message. It is on Needs you, in `todo.brain` and in Home Assistant's Repairs |

Quiet hours hold messages and do not drop them. A held message arrives when quiet hours
end, unless its problem resolved in the meantime. [Notifications →](/brain/notifications/)

## Reading what a device is, in any language

Several checks need to know what a device is: a washer, a shutoff valve, an outdoor
thermometer, a battery you charge. Once a night brAIn reads the names, makes, models and
rooms of those devices and records what each one measures and controls. It works for names
in any language, so a `hoofdkraan` is recognised as a main water valve and gets into the
leak playbook.

- A device is read again only when it changes, is renamed or moves.
- Anything brAIn is unsure about falls back to a built-in list of English words.
- A reading can make an unclassified binary sensor a safety sensor, only when brAIn is very
  sure. It can **never** stop a leak, smoke, gas or CO sensor being one.
- It never changes your protected list. The instant alert when a detector trips keys only on
  Home Assistant's own device class.

## New checks run out of sight first

A new check runs in **shadow** before it reaches you. It runs on every pass and files to a
store of its own. Nothing it finds appears on Needs you, the badge, a notification or the
To-do list.

**⚙ → Diagnostics** shows what a shadow check has been finding. Nothing is promoted
automatically. `sys.update_pending` is in shadow now.
[How shadow mode works →](/brain/corpus/#shadow-mode)
