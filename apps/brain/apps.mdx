Install another BRUH app with its Home Assistant integration and brAIn can drive it. Ask in the chat, the terminal, an automation task or by voice. There is nothing to set up.

## What you can ask

| App | Ask | Voice may |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [BRUH Bambu Studio](#bruh-bambu-studio) | *"Design a 40 mm knob for a 6 mm D-shaft and print two."* | Design, print a file, pause, resume |
| [BRUH Cooking](#bruh-cooking) | *"Start the Joule at 57 for two hours."* | Start and stop a cook |
| [BRUH Browser](#bruh-browser) | *"Add my usual order plus oat milk to the cart."* | Start a cart errand, never buy |
| [GardenRX](#gardenrx) | *"Log two pounds of tomatoes."* | Log a harvest, a spray, a note |
| [BRight](#bright) | *"Start the Friday party on the den speaker."* | Start and stop shows |
| [BRUH Print](#bruh-print) | *"Print three freezer-bag labels for bolognese."* | Print up to 10 labels |
| [BRUH Minecraft](#bruh-minecraft) | *"Teleport Alex to Sam and make it day."* | Play, not administer |
| [BRUH Task Manager](#bruh-task-manager) | *"Which app is using the most memory?"* | Read its sensors |

## Rules for every app

- **Tools appear only where the app is.** A house without BRUH Bambu Studio is offered no `bambu_*` tool, on any channel.
- **The app decides.** Each tool calls the app's own Home Assistant services, and the app says no when it should. BRUH Browser has no integration, so brAIn calls its API with a token the app writes.
- **The usual checks apply.** An agent's **Blocked services** list applies, so you can take `bruh_minecraft.teleport` away from the kitchen speaker. [Protected entities](/brain/safety/#protected-entities) are refused. A speaker or scene named to a voice agent has to be one that agent can see.
- **Voice may use, and may not administer.** Each section lists what an agent at the default **Voice assistant** level can do. The rest needs **Whole house** or **Full admin**, or the chat. [Voice →](/brain/voice/)
- **Faults are reported.** With the [development loop](/brain/development-loop/) on, each app's stopped service, log errors and integration errors are reported under the app's name.

## BRUH Bambu Studio

Bambu Lab printers over LAN, and Bambu Studio in the sidebar. [BRUH Bambu Studio →](/bruh-bambu-studio/)

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `bambu_status` | Each printer's state, progress, minutes left, layer, temperatures, speed, light and AMS trays, plus the latest print and slice jobs. Can list the files in `/share/bambu`. Read-only. |
| `bambu_print` | Start a file (an unsliced project is sliced first), or a Printables or `.stl`/`.3mf` link; pause, resume or stop the current print. |
| `bambu_control` | Speed (silent to ludicrous), chamber light, nozzle or bed target, home the axes, up to 32 lines of G-code. Temperature and G-code are refused while printing. |
| `bambu_slice` | Slice a file with Bambu Studio's own command line. |
| `bambu_design` | Design a printable part from a sentence (*"a wall hook 4 cm long, two screw holes"*) in the app's Design tab, change it, and print it. |

Ask: *"Print the cable clip on the X1"*, *"how long is left on the print?"*, *"design a 40 mm knob for a 6 mm D-shaft and print two"*.

While a print runs, the app watches its camera and brAIn looks for a failure.

**Voice** may design a part and start a print (a file, never a link; at most 4 copies), pause
and resume, and change speed, light and temperature. It may not stop a print, slice, send
G-code, open Bambu Studio or import a link.

## BRUH Cooking

Breville+ and Sage appliances: the Joule sous vide, Joule Turbo and Joule Oven Air Fryer Pro.
BRUH Cooking needs brAIn. [BRUH Cooking →](/bruh-cooking/)

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `cooking_status` | Each appliance's state (preheating, ready and waiting for the food, cooking, done, keeping warm, waiting for Start), current and target temperature, minutes left, the oven's mode and door, and the presets it can cook. Read-only. |
| `cooking_control` | Start a cook by preset (*steak medium rare*) or temperature and time, with the oven's mode, convection and level; stop it; or say the food is in or out. |

Ask: *"Start the Joule at 57 for two hours"*, *"preheat the oven to 200 on air fry"*. Name appliances the way you say them.

A sous vide must be in water, and the oven waits for Start on its own panel. brAIn tells you both. A start over a running cook is refused.

**Voice** may start and stop a cook and say the food is in or out. The appliances themselves are the last check.

## BRUH Browser

A real Chrome with Claude in Chrome, signed in to your shops, for errands such as a grocery
cart. BRUH Browser needs brAIn and runs on amd64 only. [BRUH Browser →](/bruh-browser/)

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `browser_task` | Start an errand in your words: *"add my usual order plus oat milk to the cart"*. It never buys: it builds the cart and stops on the review page. |
| `browser_status` | How an errand is going: progress, the report, and the cart with every item, price, fee and the total. |
| `browser_answer` | Answer a question a paused errand asked (*"use the 1 L carton"*). |
| `browser_stop` | Stop an errand. Nothing already in a cart is removed. |
| `browser_purchase` | Buy a cart, only after you heard it and said to buy it. |

<Aside type="caution" title="A purchase is always a second step you approve">
`browser_purchase` is always asked in the chat and the terminal, whether **Let brAIn act without asking** is on or off. It is refused anywhere nobody can be asked. The items and the total must match the cart as it stands, the cart must be under 30 minutes old, and it is bought once.
</Aside>

**Voice** may start a cart errand and read how it went. It may never buy or stop one.

## GardenRX

The spray decision engine for an orchard and garden. [GardenRX →](/gardenrx/)

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `garden_status` | This season's harvest by crop, recent picks, the last and queued sprays, pre-harvest intervals still running, and products low in stock. Read-only. |
| `garden_harvests` | What was picked, with totals per crop, for one crop or all, between two dates. Read-only. |
| `garden_log_harvest` | *"Two pounds of tomatoes"* logged as a pick. |
| `garden_log_spray` | A spray that was made, which starts each crop's pre-harvest interval. |
| `garden_log_note` | A note about a bed, a tree or an area. |
| `garden_queue_spray` | Put a spray on the queue for later. |
| `garden_adjust_inventory` | Change a product's stock. |
| `garden_add_crop` | Add a crop, or a variety of one. |

Ask: *"Log two pounds of tomatoes"*, *"how many apples did we get this year?"*. Each pass of the house checks turns the season's harvest into facts brAIn can recall, so the second question works in any conversation.

**Voice** may log a harvest, a spray and a note. Queueing a spray, changing inventory and adding
a crop need a wider agent.

## BRight

The music-driven light show director. [BRight →](/bright/)

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `bright_status` | What BRight is doing, the saved sets by name, and the tracks with a show ready. Read-only. |
| `bright_show` | Start a saved party or party mode, play one track's show, stop and put the room back (or run a scene), or import an edited show file. |

Ask: *"Start the Friday party on the den speaker"*, *"stop the lights and put the bedtime scene on"*.

**Voice** may start and stop shows. Importing an edited show needs a wider agent.

## BRUH Print

DYMO LabelWriter labels. [BRUH Print →](/bruh-print/)

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `label_printer_status` | Which printer is attached, what stock is in each roll, the saved templates and their fields, and the last few labels. Read-only. |
| `print_label` | Print text, fitted as large as it will go, or a saved template with its fields. |

Ask: *"Print a label that says chili"*, *"three freezer-bag labels for bolognese"*. brAIn reads what is loaded first, so it names a stock and a template that exist.

**Voice** prints at most 10 copies at once, because a misheard "two hundred" is a roll.

## BRUH Minecraft

A Minecraft Java server with Bedrock players through Geyser. [BRUH Minecraft →](/bruh-minecraft/)

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `minecraft_status` | Who is online, the world, version and TPS, where each player is, the time and weather, and the recent feed. |
| `minecraft_teleport` | Move a player to another player or to coordinates. |
| `minecraft_player` | Game mode or give an item; op, kick, ban and the whitelist. |
| `minecraft_world` | Time, weather and a message to everyone; the companion's building and talking. |
| `minecraft_command` | Any console command. |
| `minecraft_server` | Back up, restart or stop the server. |
| `minecraft_addons` | Search, install and remove Modrinth plugins, data packs and mods. |
| `minecraft_job` | A companion job's progress. |

Ask: *"Who's on?"*, *"teleport Alex to Sam"*, *"put Alex in creative"*, *"make it day"*.

Names are matched the way you say them. Case, spaces and a Bedrock player's `.` are ignored, and a unique partial name counts. A name that could be two people is asked about, never guessed.

brAIn can also play in the world as a companion, under the name you gave it in ⚙ › Personality. Set it up in **⚙ › Minecraft**.

**Voice** may play: teleport, game modes, items, time, weather and chat. Op, ban, kick, the
whitelist, console commands, backups, restarts and installing add-ons need a wider agent.

## BRUH Task Manager

Per-app CPU, memory, network and disk from the Supervisor, and a runaway-app sensor.
[BRUH Task Manager →](/bruh-task-manager/)

There are no dedicated tools. Task Manager's sensors are ordinary Home Assistant entities, so brAIn reads them like any other: *"which app is using the most memory?"*. An automation can hand a runaway app to brAIn with [`brain.run_task`](/brain/automations/#run-a-background-job).

## Not BRUH, also supported

brAIn also manages two other apps in depth:

- [ESPHome](/brain/esphome/): edit, validate, compile, flash and read logs.
- [Music Assistant](/brain/music-assistant/): players, the library, and the stale speakers Home Assistant cannot delete.
