<Aside type="caution" title="Experimental · requires brAIn">
BRUH Cooking is marked **experimental**, and it is built to be driven by
[brAIn](/brain/): the add-on **won't start without brAIn installed**. It uses Breville's
cloud, the same one the Breville+ Cooking app uses; it is unofficial, and isn't affiliated
with or endorsed by Breville.
</Aside>

## What it is

BRUH Cooking puts your Breville+ and Sage cooking appliances into Home Assistant and hands
them to brAIn. Start and stop cooks, change the temperature and timer, fill in a preset,
and get told when the water is ready or the food is done — from the brAIn chat, by voice,
from an automation, or from a sidebar panel that draws each appliance as it really is.

## Use cases

**Dinner that starts on the drive home.** At 5:30 pm an automation heats the Joule to
57 °C and holds it there. When the water is up to temperature your phone says *"The water
is at temperature. Add the food and press Continue."* You drop the bag in, press Continue
on the dashboard, and the timer starts.

**Hands covered in flour.** *"Hey, set the oven to bake at 350 for 25 minutes."* brAIn
starts the cook; the oven waits for you to press Start on it, so nothing heats with nobody
there. *"How long is left on the oven?"* — *"Twelve minutes; it finishes at 6:42."*

**Ten minutes' warning.** An automation fires ten minutes before **Cook end**, so the sides
are ready when the steak is.

**"What can I make in the Joule?"** brAIn reads the presets each appliance can cook — steak
from rare to well done, chicken breast, salmon, pork — with their temperatures and times
in your unit, and starts the one you pick.

**The forgotten bath.** The **Cooking** binary sensor has been on for six hours. A
reminder asks whether anyone meant to leave it running; *"stop the sous vide"* ends it.

## Supported appliances

| Appliance | Model | Status |
|---|---|---|
| ChefSteps Joule Sous Vide | CSJ100 | Tested on a real appliance |
| Joule Turbo Sous Vide | BSV600 | Built from the app's code; untested — monitor-only until you allow control |
| Joule Oven Air Fryer Pro | BOV950 | Tested on a real oven: all 15 modes start; bake, air fry and broil run to the end, with Continue after preheating and Keep warm. Cooks started on the oven itself show up and can be stopped |

Sage-branded appliances (UK, Europe and the UAE) are supported the same way, but nobody has
tried a Sage account yet. Other appliances on the account are ignored — for espresso
machines, use the [Sage Coffee](https://github.com/simonjgreen/sageha) integration.

## What you need

- **[brAIn](/brain/)**, installed and running. Install it first.
- **Home Assistant 2026.2 or newer**, on a 64-bit machine (`amd64` or `aarch64`).
- **Your Breville+ (or Sage) account** — the one you use in the Breville+ Cooking app.
  Accounts with multi-factor authentication aren't supported.
- **The appliance on Wi-Fi**, set up in the app. A Joule set to "Bluetooth only" can't be
  reached.
