BRUH Cooking puts your Breville+ and Sage cooking appliances into Home Assistant and hands
them to [brAIn](/brain/). You start, stop and watch a cook by voice, in chat, from an
automation, or from a sidebar panel that draws each appliance as it is.

![The BRUH Cooking panel's overview: a Kitchen Oven drawn mid air-fry with fries on the rack, its display reading 7:48 and Air fry 400°, and a Joule sous vide clipped in a clear container of water holding a bagged steak at 132.4° of 132.8°, both marked Cooking with the time left and when they finish](./images/overview.webp)

<Aside type="caution" title="Experimental · requires brAIn">
The app **won't start without brAIn installed**. It uses Breville's cloud, the same one the
Breville+ Cooking app uses. It is unofficial and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by
Breville.
</Aside>

## What you can do

### Ask brAIn

These work in brAIn's chat and terminal, and by voice.

| You say | What happens |
|---|---|
| *"Heat the Joule for a medium-rare steak."* | brAIn starts that preset and tells you what it started. |
| *"Set the oven to air fry at 400 for 20 minutes."* | The cook is sent. The oven waits for **Start** on its own panel before it heats. |
| *"Is the water ready?"* / *"How long is left on the oven?"* | *"Twelve minutes; it finishes at 6:42."* |
| *"The food's in."* | Continue is pressed and the timer starts. |
| *"What can I make in the Joule?"* | brAIn reads the presets that appliance can cook, with temperatures and times in your unit. |
| *"Stop the oven."* | The cook stops. Stop always works. |

### Use the panel

| You do | What happens |
|---|---|
| Open **BRUH Cooking** in the sidebar | Each appliance is drawn doing what it is doing: water circulating, the bag in, the oven's display, rack, elements and fan. |
| Open an appliance | **Stop**, **Continue**, the settings for the cook, a temperature chart and presets. |
| Pick a preset and press **Start** | Built-in sous vide and oven presets fill in the settings. Administrators can save their own. |

![The oven's full view: the drawing beside the Cook card (oven temperature 370 °F, which it holds below the setpoint, mode Air fry 400°, 7:48 left ending 6:07 PM, door closed, a red Stop button) and This cook, locked while it runs, with the oven's fifteen modes, temperature and cook time](./images/oven.webp)

### Automate

Every appliance is a Home Assistant device with status, temperature, time left, cook end,
a **Cook** switch and a **Continue** button. The [reference](/bruh-cooking/reference/) has
the YAML for each of these.

| You set up | What happens |
|---|---|
| A 5:30 pm automation calling `bruh_cooking.start_cook` | The Joule heats to 57 °C and holds it until you get home. |
| A trigger on Status changing to *Ready for food* | Your phone says *"The water is at temperature. Add the food and press Continue."* |
| A trigger ten minutes before **Cook end** | The sides are ready when the steak is. |
| A reminder when **Cooking** has been on for six hours | Someone decides whether the bath was meant to be left running. |

### Know when something is wrong

When an appliance goes offline, Status says why, since when, and one thing to try. Readings
keep their last value, marked as old. Controls go unavailable. After 10 minutes the
**Problem** sensor turns on.

## Safety limits

- **The Joule Oven waits for Start on its own panel** before it heats after a remote start.
- **UL-certified Joules wait for their top button** before cooks above 85 °C.
- **Unattended brAIn runs can read but never start, stop or continue a cook.** In chat, a start or stop passes brAIn's [action gate](/brain/safety/).
- **A start over a running cook is refused.** It never replaces the cook.
- **Untested models are monitor-only** until you allow control. Stop still works.

## Supported appliances

| Appliance | Model | Status |
|---|---|---|
| ChefSteps Joule Sous Vide | CSJ100 | Tested on a real appliance |
| Joule Turbo Sous Vide | BSV600 | Built from the app's code and untested. Monitor-only until you allow control. |
| Joule Oven Air Fryer Pro | BOV950 | Tested on a real oven. All 15 modes start. Bake, air fry and broil run to the end, with Continue after preheating and Keep warm. Cooks started on the oven itself show up and can be stopped. |

Sage-branded appliances (UK, Europe and the UAE) are supported the same way, but nobody has
tried a Sage account yet. Other appliances on the account are ignored. For espresso
machines, use the [Sage Coffee](https://github.com/simonjgreen/sageha) integration.

## What you need

| | |
|---|---|
| **[brAIn](/brain/)** | Installed and running. Install it first. |
| **Home Assistant** | 2026.2 or newer, on a 64-bit machine (`amd64` or `aarch64`) |
| **Account** | Your Breville+ or Sage account, the one you use in the Breville+ Cooking app. Accounts with multi-factor authentication aren't supported. |
| **Appliance** | On Wi-Fi, set up in the app. A Joule set to "Bluetooth only" can't be reached. |
