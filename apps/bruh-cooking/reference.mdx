Everything you need to automate a cook: examples to paste, then every service, entity and
option.

## Automation examples

These assume an appliance named *Joule*.

**Heat the water before you get home, and hold it:**

```yaml
alias: Preheat the Joule
triggers:
  - trigger: time
    at: "17:30:00"
actions:
  - action: bruh_cooking.start_cook
    target:
      entity_id: switch.joule_cook
    data:
      temperature: 57
```

**Tell me when the water is ready:**

```yaml
alias: Joule ready for food
triggers:
  - trigger: state
    entity_id: sensor.joule_status
    to: ready
actions:
  - action: notify.notify
    data:
      message: The water is at temperature. Add the food and press Continue.
```

**Ten minutes before the cook ends:**

```yaml
alias: Joule almost done
triggers:
  - trigger: time
    at:
      entity_id: sensor.joule_cook_end
      offset: "-00:10:00"
actions:
  - action: notify.notify
    data:
      message: Ten minutes to go.
```

## Services

### `bruh_cooking.start_cook`

Starts a cook with the settings you give, without changing the appliance's entities.
Target the appliance's **Cook** switch.

```yaml
action: bruh_cooking.start_cook
target:
  entity_id: switch.joule_cook
data:
  temperature: 57
  duration: "01:30:00"
```

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `temperature` | Target temperature, in your Home Assistant unit. |
| `duration` | Cook time, such as `"01:30:00"`. For sous vide, leave it out to hold the temperature until you stop it. |
| `mode` | Oven only: `bake`, `air_fry`, `roast`, `broil`… |
| `convection` | Oven only: `off`, `on` or `super`. |
| `level` | Oven only: `low`, `medium` or `high` for broil; `low` or `high` for slow cook. |
| `auto_start_timer` | Start the timer once the temperature is reached. |
| `keep_warm` | Oven only: keep the food warm when the cook finishes. |

Fields you leave out come from the appliance's entities. If you set `mode`, they come from
that mode's defaults instead (apart from `auto_start_timer` and `keep_warm`).

The standard actions work too:

| Action | Effect |
|---|---|
| `switch.turn_on` on **Cook** | Starts a cook with the entities' settings |
| `switch.turn_off` on **Cook** | Stops it |
| `button.press` on **Continue** | The food is in, or out |

### The services brAIn calls

These **name the appliance** instead of targeting an entity, **answer with what happened**,
and raise with a sentence on every refusal.

```yaml
action: bruh_cooking.start
data:
  appliance: Joule
  preset: steak_medium_rare
response_variable: cook
```

`appliance` is its name (yours in Home Assistant, or the app's), its device ID or its
serial. Leave it out when the account has one appliance.

| Service | Fields | Answers |
|---|---|---|
| `bruh_cooking.get_status` | `appliance` (optional) | `appliances`: each one's state, temperatures, time left, mode, door, error, whether control is allowed, and its next cook |
| `bruh_cooking.get_presets` | `appliance` (optional) | `presets`: the built-in and saved presets, with the appliances each fits |
| `bruh_cooking.start` | `appliance`, `preset`, and the `start_cook` fields | What was started, and for the oven that it waits for Start on its panel |
| `bruh_cooking.stop` | `appliance` | Whether there was a cook to stop |
| `bruh_cooking.continue_cook` | `appliance` | Continue pressed. Refused unless the appliance is waiting for it. |

A start over a running cook is refused. It never replaces the cook. Settings given beside a
`preset` change the preset's.

## Entities

Every appliance has:

| Entity | Type | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Status** | Sensor | Idle, Preheating, Ready for food, Cooking, Done, Keeping warm, Press the button on the appliance, Error, Updating or Offline. Attributes: when it was last heard from and the last cook (start, end, temperature, time, where its settings came from, the finish time when known); while offline, why, since when and what to try. |
| **Temperature** | Sensor | The water or oven temperature. Unknown while the oven is idle. |
| **Time remaining** | Sensor | Time left on the timer. |
| **Cook end** | Sensor | When the timer will finish. |
| **Cooking** | Binary sensor | On from the start of a cook to its end, including preheating and waiting for the food. |
| **Problem** | Binary sensor | On while the appliance reports an error, or after 10 minutes out of contact (with the reason and what to try). |
| **Error** | Sensor (diagnostic) | The error, such as Low water level or Tipped over. |
| **Preheat** | Sensor | How far preheating has got, %. Never goes back during a preheat. |
| **Target temperature** | Number | The temperature for the next cook, or of the running one. |
| **Cook time** | Number | The timer in minutes. For sous vide, 0 holds the temperature until you stop it. |
| **Cook** | Switch | On starts a cook with these settings; off stops it. |
| **Continue** | Button | The food is in (starts the timer), or out. Available only while the appliance is waiting for it. |
| **Start timer automatically** | Switch (configuration) | Start the timer as soon as the temperature is reached. On at first for the oven, as in the app. |

The **oven** also has:

| Entity | Type | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Mode** | Select | Bake, Air fry, Roast, Broil, Pizza and 10 other modes. Choosing one resets the other settings to that mode's defaults. |
| **Convection** | Select | Off, On or Super, as the mode allows. |
| **Level** | Select | Broil intensity (Low, Medium, High) or slow-cook level (Low, High). |
| **Keep warm** | Switch (configuration) | Keep the food warm when the cook finishes. |
| **Frozen food** | Switch (configuration) | Cook from frozen, for Pizza, Cookies and Reheat. |
| **Door** | Binary sensor | Whether the door is open. |
| **Active mode** | Sensor | The mode of the running cook, including cooks started on the oven. |

Each account also has a **Breville account** device (*Sage account* for Sage) with one
sensor: *Connected*, *Not connected* or *Sign-in refused*. Its attributes give the number of
appliances found, the last successful poll, and any appliance on the account BRUH Cooking
doesn't drive, with the reason.

<Aside type="note" title="Entity ids don't rename themselves">
Entity ids follow the appliance's name when it was added. If you rename it in the app, the
friendly names follow at once but the ids don't, because automations use them.
**Settings → Repairs** offers to rename the ids, and only does so when you confirm.
</Aside>

## Options

### Integration options

**Settings → Devices & services → BRUH Cooking → Configure**:

| Option | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Allow control of untested appliances** | off | The Joule Turbo hasn't been tried on a real appliance. Until this is on, Home Assistant only shows its status and can stop a cook, not start or change one. |
| **Show in the sidebar** | on | Adds the panel for everyone who uses Home Assistant. Off hides this account's appliances from it. The panel leaves the sidebar once no account shows it. |

### App options

| Option | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Home Assistant integration** (`enable_ha_integration`) | on | Deploy the `bruh_cooking` integration: each appliance as a device, the sidebar panel and the `bruh_cooking.*` services. Off: copy nothing. |
| **Log level** (`log_level`) | `info` | How much the app writes to its log. |

## What the app does

The app has no page and no port. The panel and entities come from the integration it
deploys.

| When | What it does |
|---|---|
| At start | Checks the Supervisor for **brAIn**, and stops with a clear log line if brAIn isn't installed. If the Supervisor couldn't be asked, it carries on with a warning. |
| At start | Copies the `bruh_cooking` integration into `/config/custom_components` when the shipped version differs from the deployed one, and announces it for one-click setup. |
| Every ten minutes | Puts back a deleted integration folder, and stops if brAIn was removed. The integration stays in Home Assistant until you remove it. |
| A minute after start, on every change, and hourly | Logs how each account stands: connected or not, appliances found, the last good poll, anything not driven. |

**Updates.** An update brings the new integration with it. You restart Home Assistant
**only when the integration's code changed**. Until you do, Repairs says *Restart Home
Assistant to finish updating BRUH Cooking*. A panel-only update is picked up with a page
reload.

## Data updates

| | |
|---|---|
| **Changes** | Pushed by Breville's cloud over one open connection, usually within a second or two |
| **Reconnecting** | By itself: first after 5 seconds, then less often, up to 5 minutes apart |
| **The appliance list** | Checked every **30 minutes**. New appliances appear by themselves. Ones that left the account become unavailable and can be deleted from their device page. |

## Languages

The integration's screens, entity names and messages, and the panel, follow your Home
Assistant language: English, German, French, Dutch, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, Italian,
Polish and Swedish. The printing in the drawings (the oven's display) stays as it is on the
appliance.

## Known limitations

- The Joule Turbo (BSV600) hasn't been tried on a real appliance. It is monitor-only unless
  you allow control. Its Turbo mode isn't offered.
- A running oven cook's temperature and time can't be changed. The Breville+ app can't
  either.
- Nobody has tried a Sage account yet. Accounts with multi-factor authentication can't log
  in.
- The oven's Toast, Bagel and Crispy reheat modes, Crispier air fry and Convection broil
  can't be started from Home Assistant. Active mode still shows them.
- The BOV970 and BOV870 ovens aren't supported. The Breville+ app has no controls for them
  yet.
- New appliances can take up to 30 minutes to appear.
- There's no local control. Breville can change its cloud at any time. For Bluetooth control
  of a CSJ100, see [ChefSteps Joule Sous Vide](https://github.com/acato/ha-joule).

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to do |
|---|---|
| The app stops at start and says brAIn is required | Install and start brAIn, then start BRUH Cooking again. |
| Entities are unavailable | Home Assistant can't reach Breville's cloud and keeps trying. If Status shows *Offline* instead, the appliance itself is off or disconnected. Check it in the Breville+ app. Status says why and what to try. |
| Asked to log in again | The Breville session expired. Follow the prompt in Settings → Devices & services. |
| A new appliance is missing | It appears within 30 minutes. Or go to Settings → Devices & services → BRUH Cooking → ⋮ → **Reload**. |
| A removed appliance is still listed | Once it's gone from your account, delete it from its device page. |
| Starting a cook says it hasn't been tested | Turn on **Allow control of untested appliances** (above). |
| Repairs says restart Home Assistant | The integration's code changed in an update. Restart once. |
| Anything else | Settings → Devices & services → BRUH Cooking → **Enable debug logging**, reproduce it, then **Disable debug logging** to download the log. |

### Diagnostics

When you report a problem (or ask brAIn to, with its development loop on), attach
**⋮ → Download diagnostics**. It holds each appliance's raw state, its last 50 messages from
Breville's cloud and Home Assistant's last 20 requests.

Your email, name and account IDs, login tokens, appliance names, serial numbers and network
addresses are removed wherever they appear. Still, look it over before posting it publicly.
