BRUH Browser lets [brAIn](/brain/) do things on websites for you. It runs a real Google
Chrome, signed in as you, in the Home Assistant sidebar. brAIn sends an errand, and you
watch it happen, one at a time.

![A finished errand on the BRUH Browser page: "Add my usual groceries plus oat milk to the cart at Walmart", marked Cart only — no purchase, from brAIn, in this app's Chrome, 41 steps. The report says blueberries were out of stock and one oat milk was swapped for another; the cart lists each item with quantity and price, delivery, tax and a 64.37 USD total, the substitution and the item left out, and a green box saying nothing has been bought, with Buy this and Don't buy](./images/cart.webp)

<Aside type="caution" title="Experimental · requires brAIn · amd64 only">
Running Claude in Chrome inside a container, driven from a script, is a path Anthropic's
documentation does not cover. BRUH Browser fails loudly and says which setup step to redo,
but the first real run on your machine may still find something that needs a fix.
</Aside>

## What you can do

### Shop

| You say or do | What happens |
|---|---|
| *"Add my usual Walmart order plus oat milk."* | Chrome opens the store and fills the cart from your shopping profile and the store's buy-again list. It stops on the review page. |
| Wait | brAIn tells you: *"The cart is ready: 64.37 with delivery tomorrow 8–9 am. Blueberries were out of stock."* |
| *"Buy it"*, in brAIn's chat or terminal | brAIn shows an approval card with the store, items and total. After you approve, a second errand places the order. |
| Press **Buy this** under the cart, then confirm the total | The same second errand places the order. |
| Press **Don't buy** | That cart can never be bought. |

### Answer it when it needs you

| It stops because | You do |
|---|---|
| Two products fit, or one is out of stock | Press the option you want, type something else, or tell brAIn *"use the frozen ones"*. |
| The store wants a login, a code or a CAPTCHA | Do it in the live browser on the page, then press **I've done it, carry on**. |
| You change your mind | Press **Stop this errand**. |

Which should I add instead?" — notes that the answer changes what is spent and nothing is bought until you approve the finished cart, and offers three buttons (raspberries, frozen blueberries, leave them out), a box to tell it what to do instead with Send, and Stop this errand](./images/asking.webp)

### Teach it your usual

| You say or do | What happens |
|---|---|
| *"Look at my old Walmart orders and learn what I usually buy."* | An order-history errand saves a shopping profile for that store: items, sizes, how often, brands you prefer, things to avoid. |
| Press **Remove** beside an item | It stays out of the profile, even when a later errand sees it again. |

### Look things up

| You say | What happens |
|---|---|
| *"Find the cheapest 1 kg spool of white PETG."* | An errand searches, reads the results and reports back. No cart, no purchase. |

## It never buys without your yes

- **A purchase is always a second errand.** The first builds the cart and stops. The second runs only after a person has seen that cart and approved it.
- **The approval is checked every time.** It must name this cart, built under 30 minutes ago, never approved before, with the total you approved.
- **Voice cannot buy.** Voice can start a cart errand, ask how it went, and answer a choice that isn't about money.
- **Nothing is bought on another computer.** Purchases run only in this app's own Chrome.
- **It never types a password or card number**, changes account, address or payment settings, accepts new terms or subscriptions, or follows instructions written on a web page.

The [full list of limits](/bruh-browser/guide/#what-it-will-not-do) is in the guide.

## What you need

| | |
|---|---|
| **[brAIn](/brain/)** | Installed and running. BRUH Browser refuses to start without it. |
| **A paid Claude plan** | Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise. Claude in Chrome does not work with an API key. |
| **Two one-time sign-ins** | The Claude extension in the browser and Claude Code inside this app, with the same Claude account |
| **Machine** | amd64 (x86-64) only. Google publishes Chrome for Linux on x86-64 only, so there is no Raspberry Pi version. |
| **Home Assistant** | 2024.7 or newer |
