<Aside type="caution" title="Experimental · requires brAIn · amd64 only">
Running Claude in Chrome inside a container, driven by Claude Code from a script, is a path
Anthropic's documentation does not cover. BRUH Browser is built to fail loudly and say which
setup step to redo, but the first real run on your machine may still find something that
needs a fix — brAIn's development loop can report it.
</Aside>

## What it is

**This app exists for [brAIn](/brain/).** It runs a real Google Chrome, with Anthropic's
**Claude in Chrome** extension, on a virtual screen inside Home Assistant — so that brAIn can
do things on websites for you. On its own it does nothing useful: brAIn sends the errands,
and this app carries them out where you can watch, one at a time, in the sidebar.

## It never buys without your yes

An errand **builds the cart and stops** on the store's review page. brAIn reads you every
item, quantity, price, fee, the delivery slot and the total. Only when you say *"buy it"*
does brAIn ask you to approve the purchase — an approval card with the cart in front of you
— or you press **Buy this** under the cart on this app's page. Only then does a **second
errand** re-read the checkout and place the order, and only if the cart still matches what
you approved, within 30 minutes of it being built. **Voice can build a cart and ask how it
went; it cannot buy.**

## Use cases

**The weekly shop, by voice.** *"Hey, add my usual Walmart order plus oat milk."* brAIn
starts an errand; Chrome opens the store, works from your shopping profile and the store's
buy-again list, and fills the cart. Twenty minutes later brAIn tells you: *"The cart is
ready: 64.37 with delivery tomorrow 8–9 am. Blueberries were out of stock."* At
your desk you open the cart, read it, and say *"buy it"* — brAIn shows the approval card
with the items and total, you approve, and a second errand places the order.

**A substitution, asked properly.** Mid-errand it stops: two products both fit, or one is
out of stock. The page — and brAIn — shows the choices with their prices. *"Use the frozen
ones."* It carries on.

**A login only you can do.** The store wants a one-time code. The errand stops and points
you at the live browser; you type the code, press **I've done it, carry on**, and it picks up
where it stopped.

**Price checks and research.** *"Find the cheapest 1 kg spool of white PETG."* An errand
searches, reads the results and reports back — no cart, no purchase.

**"What do I usually buy?"** *"Look at my old orders and learn what I usually buy."* An
order-history errand reads what the store shows and saves a profile you can review and
prune on the page.

## What you need

- **[brAIn](/brain/)**, installed and running. BRUH Browser refuses to start without it.
- **A paid Claude plan** — Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise. Claude in Chrome does not work with
  an API key.
- **Two one-time sign-ins with the same Claude account**: the Claude extension in the
  browser, and Claude Code inside this app.
- **An amd64 (x86-64) machine.** Google publishes Chrome for Linux on x86-64 only, so there
  is no Raspberry Pi version.
- Home Assistant 2024.7 or newer.
