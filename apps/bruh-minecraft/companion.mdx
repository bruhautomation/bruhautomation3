BRUH Minecraft is a server first: with nothing else installed it runs no companion, shows none and
logs nothing about one. Install [brAIn](/brain/) and a **companion** joins the family in the world,
under the name you gave brAIn in ⚙ › Personality ("brAIn" if you gave none). It appears as a small
named **allay** beside whoever it is working for.

## What it is like to play with

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Just talk" icon="comment-alt">
    Everything the family types in chat is for it (the default), and it stays quiet for chatter
    like "lol" and players talking to each other. *"Build Sam a little bridge"*, *"make the roof
    red"*, *"put a couch in this room"*.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Point with a spyglass" icon="magnifier">
    Everyone gets a named spyglass on joining. Point it at something and say nothing, and the
    companion offers two or three ideas on choice pads; point and then talk, and "this" means what
    you pointed at.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Questions without reading" icon="star">
    For children who do not read yet: two or three coloured pads appear beside them, each with a
    picture and an item floating over it. Standing on one for two seconds is the answer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Big requests become jobs" icon="rocket">
    A build, a room made over or a tree runs as a **job** while you keep playing — up to five at
    once. You see its progress over your hotbar and hear one line when it is done.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

Something quick is done at once. Asking about a running job changes it, and *"stop the bridge"*
stops it **and puts back what it changed**. A message longer than one chat line can be sent in
parts — `1 build a stone tower`, `2 with a spiral staircase`, `3 and a red roof`, then `end`
(`cancel` drops it).

### Seeing what it is thinking

While it works for you, a small **thought bubble** floats over its allay with what it is doing right
now — *"looking at the blocks around you"*, *"placing 340 blocks"* — and a line per job with how far
along it is. The allay hovers by what it is building (or circles you while it only thinks), and
everyone in the game can see it. A quick answer never shows a bubble, and nothing goes in chat.
Use the **journal** book named after the companion to read everything: what it is doing now, every
job, and its recent steps. A long job can also show a **boss bar** at the top of the screen, which
Bedrock players see too.

The bubble and the journal need **BRUH Sight** (below), so a Paper or Purpur server. On other server
types steps of work show on the action bar instead.

## Setting it up

The companion belongs to brAIn: who is family, bedtime, limits and the models are set in **brAIn ›
⚙ › Minecraft** (the section appears when BRUH Minecraft is installed).

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **On or off**, and **say hello** | Whether it plays, and whether it greets the family on joining |
| **Answer every line from the family** | On by default; off, only lines that start with its name |
| **Give everyone a spyglass** | On by default |
| **Show what it is thinking** | The bubble and journal; on by default |
| **Jobs at once** | 1–5, 5 by default. More wait their turn, and so does a job in the same place as one already running |
| **The family** | Each person as Child or Grown-up, with their in-game names (Bedrock names with the dot) and any nickname a server script shows. Anybody not on the list is left alone |
| **Bedtime** | A switch (or a door sensor) that means bedtime: children get a goodnight and nothing new starts for them |
| **Limits** | Turns a day, things it does a day, and things it does for one player in a sitting. 0 means no limit, which is where they start |
| **Models** | One for answering in chat, one for building |
| **Can ask for anything** | Per grown-up, off by default — see below |
| **What it did** | Everything it did in the world, with **Undo** |

On the panel's Dashboard, the **Companion** card carries the companion's name and shows what it is
doing, the **protected places**, a question to put on the pads, and everything it did with
**Undo**. Without brAIn the card is one line: *"Install brAIn to have a companion play in the
world."*

## What it may do

Everyone on the family list — children too — may ask for anything **in the game**: build, change
blocks (saved first, so Undo puts them back), look around, ask a question on the pads, time and
weather. Anything outside the game is answered *"I can't do that from the game"*. A child is never
told about another child.

**Can ask for anything** turns a grown-up's game chat into brAIn's chat: game modes, items,
teleports, any console command, any block anywhere — and the house, following brAIn's permission
setting (with *act without asking* off, a house change waits for a Yes in brAIn and on the phone).
It is only used while the **server checks accounts** (`online-mode` on, or Bedrock players signed in
through Floodgate): an unchecked name could be anybody. Children never get it.

<Aside title="Held next to the world, whoever asks">
The add-on itself enforces the companion's limits in code: it only ever summons its own allay;
plays only gentle sounds, quietly, to one player; removes only its own entities; builds on its own
only into empty space on natural ground, never in a **protected place** or next to a build; backs
the world up before anything large; and logs every action with an Undo. A *trusted edit* for a
grown-up with *Can ask for anything* may change any block, and every block's state is saved first
so Undo still puts it all back.
</Aside>

## BRUH Sight

On **Paper and Purpur** the add-on builds and installs a small plugin of its own, **BRUH Sight**. It
answers the panel from the running server over a private local connection (loopback only, with a
token), instead of from RCON polls and the saved world files.

- **Where everybody is** updates every second instead of every five.
- Each player comes with the **block and creature under their crosshair**, what they hold and carry,
  what is around them, and the blocks they just placed or broke.
- **Looking at blocks**, an edit's undo snapshot and the spyglass read the world **as it is now**,
  including builds the server has not saved yet.
- It draws the companion's **thought bubble** and **journal**, and sees where the companion is
  actually building so its allay goes there.

BRUH Sight changes no block. On Vanilla, Fabric, Forge and Folia the panel reads the world the
older way, and everything still works without the bubble.

## The world atlas

In the background the add-on reads the saved world, a few chunks a second and nearest the players
first, and works out its **buildings**, the **rooms** inside them (floor, free floor space, doors,
windows, light, furniture, the blocks they are made of, and a guess at what each room is), the
**roads** between them, and names from **signs**. Name anything with `label_place` (*"this is Sam's
room"*). That is how *"put a couch in this room"* works without coordinates.

## More things it can do

- **Maps of your builds** — `render_maps` draws finished maps from the saved world and puts them in
  a player's inventory, named A1, A2, B1… so they hang edge to edge in item frames.
- **Skins** — `set_skin` puts a skin on a player through SkinsRestorer and waits for its answer.
- **Scripts** — brAIn can write a Skript script or a data-pack function into the running world,
  load it without a restart and hear any errors (`write_script`, `list_scripts`,
  `remove_script`). It never touches a file it did not write.
- **Rename the world** — *"rename the world to Our World"*.

## For your own automations

The companion is built on a public contract: the add-on **tells** Home Assistant what happens as
events (`bruh_minecraft_chat`, `_join`, `_leave`, `_death`, `_advancement`, `_place`, `_answer`,
`_point`) and **does** what it is asked through services (`companion_say`, `ask_choice`,
`companion_build`, `undo_action`, …). It decides nothing on its own, so your own automations can
use the same lane. See [Home Assistant Integration](/bruh-minecraft/home-assistant/#game-events).
