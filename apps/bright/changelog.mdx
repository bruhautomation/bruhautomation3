A condensed history. Patch releases that only polished the panel are grouped with the release they
followed; the add-on's own `CHANGELOG.md` has every line.

<Changelog>

<Release version="0.24.3" date="November 24, 2026" latest>
<Changes type="changed">

- **Party** says *Latest* in full on each song's version picker, and a song with one show says so.
- **Test mode** groups bulbs by zone in closed folds, each with a tick for the whole zone and a count.
- **Light Map: Suggest zones from names** proposes zones from a word the lights' names share, and saves them only when you press **Use these zones**.
- **Shows** says *"Pick a song above to open its show"* when none is open; **Library** says how many tracks are ready and how many still need analysing.
- On a phone the tab strip fades at an edge with more tabs past it, and the open tab scrolls into view.

</Changes>
</Release>

<Release version="0.24.2" date="November 23, 2026">
<Changes type="fixed">

- Every row of the Party set list has the same layout, whether a song has several shows, one, or none yet.
- The Shows page opens with one summary line, lists eight songs with **Show all**, and tapping a song scrolls to its show.

</Changes>
</Release>

<Release version="0.24.1" date="November 23, 2026">
<Changes type="fixed">

- Library, Shows and Party show each song's length, and a short clip and the full song it was cut from are marked as a pair.
- The Light Map list explains zones, counts unplaced lights, groups lights by zone and has a search box.
- Test mode starts with the bulbs this browser last tested with, not every light in the house.

</Changes>
</Release>

<Release version="0.24.0" date="November 17, 2026">
<Changes type="changed">

- **The shared BRUH panel look**: one centred page column, a clear button hierarchy (one main action per card), one-grid effect forms, and on a phone a single scrolling row of tabs.

</Changes>
</Release>

<Release version="0.23.x">

Plain words on every tab, and two new services.

<Changes type="added">

- **`bright.import_show`** loads an edited show file from `/config/.bright/shows`, with a `README.md` and an `EFFECTS.md` beside the files.
- **`bright.get_status`** answers what is playing, the saved sets and the tracks with a show ready; `parties_only` skips the library scan.

</Changes>
<Changes type="changed">

- Shows read in plain words (*"Written by Claude · 2032 cues · 3 min ago"*, *"Ready · played 3 d ago"*) with one visible action and the rest behind ⋯.
- The bulb checks list each bulb in one compact row (*"Fast, 42 ms, no loss"*), with **Probe all unprobed**.
- The Library folds its folders into one line, and songs show their own title and artist, with duplicates marked *"Same song as …"*.
- The show director's answer is checked against a schema; the mood comes first in what Claude is told.
- The add-on says when Home Assistant needs a restart for a new integration, and only then. Marked **experimental** in the store.

</Changes>
<Changes type="fixed">

- With no calibrated speaker, the pickers say so and offer **Calibrate a speaker first**; Play stays off until there is one.
- Light Map names are readable in both themes; lights sharing a spot show as one dot with a count.
- Phones no longer zoom into text boxes or scroll the page sideways.
- Speaker-name lookups give up after 2 seconds on a slow Home Assistant instead of 15.
- Starting and stopping parties and shows from brAIn works.

</Changes>
<Changes type="security">

- The show director's requests to brAIn ask for read-only tools.
- Track paths from a request are normalised and confined to `/media`.

</Changes>
</Release>

<Release version="0.22.0">

**Manual mode has a transport — an instrument you can keep time with.**

<Changes type="changed">

- **The song's own grid is the clock**, with a transport showing BPM, bar and beat, or a tapped tempo with a downbeat reset.
- **Loops are clips**, recorded the way a DAW records them: choose 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars and a snap, count in, and the loop closes itself on the bar line.

</Changes>
<Changes type="fixed">

- A Manual session now powers the room on and lays down a dim base first — a switched-off bulb accepted every packet and showed none of them.
- Live gestures no longer wait in the rate limiter.

</Changes>
</Release>

<Release version="0.21.0">
<Changes type="changed">

- **The map is the instrument.** The Manual tab is one phone screen; gestures ride one live connection, and late work is dropped rather than queued.

</Changes>
</Release>

<Release version="0.20.0">
<Changes type="added">

- **Manual — play the lights yourself.** A new tab: tap bulbs, record loops, DROP, FLASH and a rack of effects, with the room restored when you stop.

</Changes>
</Release>

<Release version="0.19.0">

**Test mode, and a show that never loses the beat.**

<Changes type="added">

- **🧪 Test mode — beats & drops** in the Lab: a song plays while the ticked bulbs pulse on the beat and flash on every drop.

</Changes>
<Changes type="changed">

- There is always a beat on some light, through intros and quiet sections too. A drop is the whole room.

</Changes>
<Changes type="fixed">

- A party's end scene no longer fires between songs, and the run state no longer drops to idle between tracks.
- A show is stoppable from its first second; a Stop racing the play command no longer leaves the music playing.
- Party lights turn off when their cue ends, not when the track does.

</Changes>
</Release>

<Release version="0.18.x">

**Lights with an attack, four layers, and the song on screen.**

<Changes type="added">

- **`hit` and `accent`**: strikes that are at full brightness the instant they land and decay from there — on the drums the analyser actually heard.
- **The song is drawn** in the show editor: chords, melody and drums on the waveform's ruler, and one named row per effect drawn from what was compiled.
- **Show versions**: every compile, revision and hand edit is kept; one is live, and a named version is kept for good.
- **The tune runs across the room** — `melody` places each note by its pitch. A party can pin which version of a show each song plays.

</Changes>
<Changes type="changed">

- **The automatic director builds four layers on different lights** — ground, pulse, hits and voice — and Claude's brief teaches the same model.
- **One party mode**: tick the shows you want and press Play. The vibe moved to the Shows tab.

</Changes>
<Changes type="fixed">

- **The beat pulse peaked on the off-beat** in every show since the first release; every shape now lands its peak on the beat.
- The drum detector found no accents in ordinary music, and could not tell a kick from a snare. Existing libraries are re-analysed.
- An effect whose selection matches no light now says so.

</Changes>
</Release>

<Release version="0.17.0">

**Colour that moves without flickering, and lights that follow the audio.**

<Changes type="added">

- **`colour_drift`** (the hue walks, brightness never moves), **`saturate`** (colour swell) and **`level`** (brightness follows the audio itself, per band) — and the automatic show uses all three.

</Changes>
<Changes type="fixed">

- Two bulb-run effects on one light cancelled each other silently; the editor now says so.

</Changes>
</Release>

<Release version="0.16.x">

**The analyser learns to hear the music.**

<Changes type="added">

- **Harmony, melody, phrases and repetition** are analysed, and **`melody`** and **`harmony`** follow them.
- **Test the Claude director** on the Shows tab, and **Or suggest some** on the Effects tab.

</Changes>
<Changes type="fixed">

- An upgraded analyser now re-hears a library that was analysed before it.
- Asking Claude for a show is a background job, so a long compile no longer fails with *"load failed"*.

</Changes>
</Release>

<Release version="0.15.0">

**Stabs on the beat, notes to the director, and a party you can steer.**

<Changes type="added">

- The analyser ranks the song's hits, and `"snap": "beat"` moves a moment onto the nearest beat.
- **Notes for the director** and **Revise with Claude** in the show editor.
- **`director_model`** option (default `opus`).
- **⏮ Prev / ⏭ Next** and **🎤 Sync by ear** while a party plays.

</Changes>
</Release>

<Changes type="added">
