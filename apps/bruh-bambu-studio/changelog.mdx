<Changelog>

<Release version="0.5.3" latest>

<Changes type="changed">

- **Failure detection stops taking looks it cannot use.** After one look comes back blind with the chamber light off, it holds, says so on the Printers tab with a **Turn the light on** button, and looks again as soon as the light comes on. With *Turn the chamber light on to see* set, it turns the light on once and carries on; a print starting in the dark with that setting off is warned about before the first look.
- **Each printer's header carries its state** (Printing, Finished, Ready) and, under the name, the file with its progress or "finished 2 h ago", in the same words the dashboard card uses.
- **Every Files row has Print in the same place** and a slice line ("1 h 30 min · 31 g", or "Not sliced yet"); a 3MF and an STL of the same model sit together.
- The card's failure-detection line shows a whole sentence without cutting it off.

</Changes>

</Release>

<Release version="0.5.2">

<Changes type="fixed">

- A print name whose characters the screen cannot draw no longer ends in empty brackets.
- The Files list is quieter, and the same model kept as STL, 3MF and STEP shows its format on each row even when the names differ in case or punctuation.
- The card's failure-detection row agrees with itself — a check-mark shield when all is well, a warning shield with the reason when the last look saw nothing — and fits on one line on a phone.
- A warning that the last print could not be watched folds to one quiet note once no print is running.
- Look marks on the layer line no longer overlap.

</Changes>

</Release>

<Release version="0.5.1">

<Changes type="fixed">

- The dashboard card's main button, its Stop button and its warning and failure notices are readable on Home Assistant's default theme, light and dark.

</Changes>

</Release>

<Release version="0.5.0">

<Changes type="changed">

- **One page width and one left edge** for Printers, Files and Settings, with the top bar lined up at every screen size. Studio and Design stay full width.
- On a printer's card, Failure detection, On the printer and Temperatures and G-code are boxes of the same shape.
- Files is three clear parts — the library's title and Upload, the import box, and the file list with its search. On a phone, Print and ⋯ sit under a file's title.
- Spacing, text sizes and corners follow the shared BRUH standard, and the dashboard card follows the shared card layout with 44 px buttons and the theme's primary colour.

</Changes>

</Release>

<Release version="0.4.5 – 0.4.9">

<Changes type="changed">

- **Print names read as a person would write them** everywhere — no folder, extension, slicer time stamp, trailing "(1)", underscores or empty boxes — and wrap rather than being cut off.
- **Each file row says how long it prints and how much filament it uses**, or that it is not sliced yet, with its own plate picture or a clear icon for its kind. The library has a search box and sorts by newest or name.

</Changes>

<Changes type="fixed">

- Failure detection says up front when it could not watch a print and why, never shows a blind look as a green "fine" dot, and offers **Turn on light** beside the notice.
- The camera's light, pause and full-screen buttons are 40 px and nothing covers them; menus open in their own row instead of floating over the camera.
- Timeline markers are 40 px targets, spaced for a finger; the Printers page no longer scrolls sideways on a phone.
- AMS spools say their colour and material ("White PLA"), with the amount left only when the printer sends it.
- Blue buttons and the active tab are readable (5.4:1 contrast).
- The 3D view's named-view buttons wrap on a phone instead of being cut off.
- The Files list's Slice item works again.

</Changes>

</Release>

<Release version="0.4.0">

<Changes type="added">

- **A Settings tab** for the printers, failure detection's settings and where alerts go, brAIn's models and your standing instructions, the 3D view, and the Home Assistant card.
- **Choose brAIn's model** for Design conversations and for print checks.
- **Standing instructions** for brAIn: *Designing* is added to every Design conversation, *Printing* is followed whenever brAIn picks print settings.

</Changes>

<Release version="0.3.1">

<Changes type="fixed">

<Release version="0.3.0">

<Changes type="added">

- **Print failure detection.** While a print runs, brAIn looks at the camera at the moments the print's own G-code says it is most likely to fail, compares it with Bambu Studio's picture of the finished plate, warns you with **Ignore** and **Pause print**, and pauses a print that has clearly failed. *Only warn* never pauses; Relaxed, Balanced or Careful set how often it looks.
- **Free checks without brAIn**: a covered lens or dark chamber, a frozen camera, a sudden change on the plate, and the printer's own camera alerts.
- **Plate clear** after a print.
- **In Home Assistant**: Failure detection, Print problem, Plate clear and Camera covered entities, a Last print check camera, phone alerts with buttons, the `check_print`, `print_alert_action` and `set_failure_detection` services and a `bruh_bambu_print_alert` event.

</Changes>

<Changes type="fixed">

- Downloaded 3MF and STEP files are named after the part, its version and its kind.

</Changes>

</Release>
