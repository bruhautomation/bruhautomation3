## What it is

A Home Assistant add-on that puts **Claude** to work as your home's analyst. It collects a slimmed snapshot of your HA data — entity states with areas, recent history, long-term energy statistics — sends it to Claude with a strict design system, and renders the result as a self-contained interactive visualization in an **Insights** panel in your sidebar. Every card comes with sharp written analysis, highlight stats, hover tooltips, animations, light & dark mode, and a colorblind-safe palette — designed specifically for *your* data.

## Works with your Claude subscription

No API key required. Connect once with your Claude **Pro or Max** account using the guided sign-in in the panel (or paste a token from `claude setup-token`). An Anthropic API key works too if you prefer pay-as-you-go. Already running **[BRUH Terminal](/bruh-claude/)**? Run `ha-share-login` there once and Insights picks the login up automatically — one login for the whole BRUH family.

:::tip[Better together]
With BRUH Terminal installed, the connection goes far beyond login: the two share one **home memory** (what the analyst learns, your voice assistant knows), the token-budget meter uses your **real account utilization**, and the Terminal's [Power Tools](/bruh-claude/power-tools/) keep the areas, labels, and statistics that Insights analyzes clean and complete. The full story: [Terminal + Insights, better together](/bruh-claude/better-together/).
:::

## Private by design

- Home data is sent to Anthropic's API only when an insight is generated — nothing else leaves your machine.
- Person **GPS coordinates are never included** in snapshots; only zone/state and areas are.
- Visualizations render in **sandboxed iframes** — they can't touch your HA session or cookies.
- The panel is only reachable through HA **Ingress** (admin users); the optional dashboard-card server is unmapped by default and token-protected.
- The `/config` mount is **read-only**.

## Up and running in 5 minutes

1. Add the **BRUH HA Apps** repository in HA.
2. Install **BRUH Insights** and start it.
3. Click **Insights** in the sidebar and connect your Claude account.
4. Hit **Refresh all** — enjoy the show.
