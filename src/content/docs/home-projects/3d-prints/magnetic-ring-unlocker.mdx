Parents understand the constant back-and-forth with childproof latches. This magnetic ring lets adults unlock cabinets and gates instantly while still providing reliable protection against small hands.

## Design

A simple neodymium magnet embedded in a brass ring triggers the release mechanism on standard magnetic childproof latches. Wear it daily and keep one nearby for quick access.

## Hardware

- Neodymium magnet (N52, ~15mm diameter)
- Brass or stainless steel ring blank
- Two-part epoxy adhesive
- Adjustable ring base

## How It Works

Standard magnetic latches use a release magnet under a small metal catch. This ring positions the activating magnet at the right angle to reliably trigger the latch from above or the side.

## Installation

Simply press and hold the ring against the latch catch mechanism until you hear or feel a click. Most latches release instantly.
