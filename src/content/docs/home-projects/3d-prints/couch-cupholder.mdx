## Another Story

Once upon a time, during the peak of the COVID lockdown era, I moved into a new house, bought a sofa, and waited 14 weeks for it to arrive. Technically, it's a sectional, so sprawling that it could be a continent — perfect for my Great Dane, who thinks my life's purpose is to pre-warm her seat.

Sitting on my brand-new couch, I realized there wasn't a cupholder. Probably to distract myself from my buyer's remorse, I built my own.

## Design

I designed a thing that slips over the arm and has an insert to accommodate different size cups. Turns out this product already exists, so you should probably just buy it.

## Make Your Own

I printed this on my [Ender 3 V2](https://amzn.to/3ZfeX4p) using [carbon fiber PLA](https://amzn.to/48qm3aL). The surface finish with carbon fiber PLA is insane if you've never used it.

I added .stl and .step files to my [GitHub repo](https://github.com/bruhautomation/BRUH-CAD-Files) if you want to make your own. Feel free to remix to suit your preferences, couch shape, cup size, etc.
