After years of mowing at dusk, I finally built a proper lighting solution. This LED bar mounts directly to the mower deck, providing illumination ahead of the blades.

## Design

A streamlined bracket attaches the LED bar to standard mower deck holes, angling the light forward for maximum visibility. The power runs to a simple switch mounted near the engine starter, keeping controls intuitive and accessible.

## Hardware

- High-output LED bar (suitable for outdoor use)
- Aluminum mounting bracket
- Weather-resistant switch
- 12V or 24V power (depending on mower system)
- UV-resistant cable harness

## Benefits

- **Visibility:** Safe operation after sunset
- **Simple:** Works with existing mower electrical
- **Durable:** Stainless fasteners, sealed connectors
