## Background

I wanted a wall-mounted tablet for Home Assistant dashboards but couldn't find a mount that checked all the boxes: magnetic (easily removable), USB charging with a captive cable, only standard wall cutouts, universal tablet fit, and a clean look that blends in.

After a few prototypes, I landed on a design built around a keystone jack plate.

## Installation

### Finding the Right Spot

Where you install the tablet determines how easy the project will be. If you're routing the cable through the wall, find a spot that shares the same stud cavity as an outlet. In the US, studs are typically 16 inches apart, and outlets are attached to the stud on either side. Use a stud finder and look for a clear path without studs or firebreaks. Interior walls generally aren't insulated, which makes pulling wires easier.

:::caution
Don't cut into walls unless you're comfortable with what's behind the drywall.
:::

### Low-Voltage Bracket

Trace the interior outline of the bracket onto the wall. I installed mine about 55 inches off the floor. Cut out the hole with a [drywall saw](https://www.amazon.com/IRWIN-Tools-Standard-Drywall-2014102/dp/B000B3EGN8?tag=brau01-20), push in the bracket, and tighten the screws to extend the wings and secure it.

## Bezel Variant

I also designed a bezel version when helping my parents replace old Crestron CT-1000 touch panels. The setup used ancient VIZIO XR6M10 tablets with a dual-gang bracket installed into the old CT-1000 housing, powered by a [USB buck-boost step-down](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B07JHH294R?tag=brau01-20) wired to the existing power supply.

## Dashboard Setup

My tablets run the Home Assistant app and land directly on the HA dashboard. I use the [Google Photos](https://github.com/daanoz/ha-google-photos) and [WallPanel](https://github.com/j-a-n/lovelace-wallpanel) integrations to display photos as a screensaver when the tablets are idle.
