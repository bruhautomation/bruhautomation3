## A Story

Maybe it's just me, but I love well-made hardware — That "ku-thunk" of a vintage car door. The smooth "thwack" of a perfectly aligned deadbolt sliding into place. That's my jam.

So, of course, I spent an entire weekend swapping out perfectly functional doorknobs in my house. Forget sports cars or cool sneakers; my flex is high-quality door hardware.

After spiraling down the rabbit hole of doorknob reviews (yes, they exist), I found it — a modern yet timeless design, clean lines, and a solid feel. It's the little black dress of the doorknob world. Perfection.

As I marveled at the metallic beauty of my 26th doorknob replacement, my two-year-old flopped through the door like [Julius the Snake](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Bxc_55ur-J4). My wife, in pursuit, reminded me, "You know these need to be child-proofed, right?"

*Panic.* Visions filled my mind of white globs of plastic hanging on my doorknobs like some malignant marshmallow. Surely, these knobs deserve better than some plebian clamshell eyesore. Desperately, I scoured the web for an elegant solution and found nothing. So, I did what any self-respecting wanna-be maker would do — I made my own.

## The Knob In Question

If this needs clarification, I really like the [Schlage Bowery](https://amzn.to/467wrSB) doorknob. It comes in a lot of colors and configurations. In my case, I went [Matte Black with the Greyson Trim](https://amzn.to/3r8wOh0).

These door knobs are "screwless". The base installs like a standard doorknob; the trim piece is snapped into place, and then the doorknob clips onto the stem.

An ordinary child-proof doorknob acts like a slip ring you squeeze down on or poke your fingers through when turning the knob. Typically, these clamp over the door knob with a clam shell. Since the Bowery door knob clips into the stem, I made a slip ring that goes behind the knob before installation and is held captive when the knob clicks into place. I modeled and 3D printed a low-profile cover that's hardly noticeable from the front and matches the finish.

## 3D Printing

I had the best result using my MSLA 3D printer ([Elegoo Mars](https://amzn.to/3r8xZge)), but these are easily printed on a traditional FDM/FFF 3D printer. In my testing, the [water-soluble resin](https://amzn.to/453EP4R) I used was the right balance of rigid but not hard enough to scratch the door knob. It was also an exact color match, and the surface finish was ready-to-go right off the printer.
