## The Problem

I replaced all the doorknobs in my house with [Schlage Bowery](https://amzn.to/467wrSB) knobs — then had kids. Every child-proof cover I could find was a plastic clamshell that looked terrible on nice hardware. So I designed my own.

## The Knob

The Schlage Bowery comes in a lot of finishes and configurations. I went with [Matte Black with the Greyson Trim](https://amzn.to/3r8wOh0). The knobs are "screwless" — the base screws in, the trim piece snaps on, and the knob clips onto the stem.

## The Design

Standard child-proof covers act like a slip ring — you squeeze down on the outer shell to grip the knob and turn it. Since the Bowery clips onto the stem, I designed a slip ring that goes behind the knob before installation and is held captive when the knob clicks into place. The cover is low-profile and barely visible from the front.

## Printing

Best results came from an MSLA printer ([Elegoo Mars](https://amzn.to/3r8xZge)), but FDM works fine too. I used [water-soluble resin](https://amzn.to/453EP4R) which was rigid enough to function without being hard enough to scratch the knob. It also happened to be an exact color match, and the surface finish was good right off the print bed.
