A bottle holder designed to fit inside a biosafety cabinet for organizing reagents while maintaining airflow.

Inside a biosafety cabinet, reagent bottles tend to wander, and a cluttered work zone disrupts the laminar airflow that keeps both your samples and you protected. This holder corrals bottles into fixed slots so they stay put and upright, while an open, low-profile footprint keeps air moving freely through the cabinet. It's a simple print-and-use organizer — no hardware, no assembly. Print it, set it on the work surface, and load it up.

## Printing and material

Print in PLA or PETG — PETG holds up better to repeated wipe-downs with disinfectants like ethanol or dilute bleach. Print flat with no supports for a clean top surface, and favor a smoother finish over textured or porous surfaces, since it decontaminates more easily. The slot spacing is sized for common lab wash and reagent bottles; check the STEP file and adjust the slot diameter if your bottles run larger or smaller.
