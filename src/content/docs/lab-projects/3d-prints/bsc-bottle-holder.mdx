A modular storage system designed to maximize reagent bottle organization within biosafety cabinets. Keeps frequently used media and supplements accessible while freeing bench space for active work.

## Design

The rack features adjustable shelf heights to accommodate bottles from 50mL to 1L capacity. The design fits standard Class II BSC interior dimensions (typically 24" wide). Multiple racks can be stacked vertically for tall cabinets, and the lightweight structure doesn't restrict airflow.

## Hardware

- **Frame:** 3D-printed PETG with reinforced corners
- **Shelves:** Adjustable pins or grooved tracks
- **Dimensions:** ~20" W × 6" D × 8" H per rack (adjustable)
- **Capacity:** 8-12 bottles per rack depending on size
- **Material:** Chemical-resistant PETG or polycarbonate

## Features

- **Fits 50mL to 1L bottles:** adjustable shelf spacing
- **BSC standard:** designed for Class II cabinet interior
- **Stackable:** safe stacking without tools
- **Autoclavable:** supports 121°C steam sterilization (confirm material first)
- **Transparent:** clear visibility of bottle levels
- **Lightweight:** doesn't obstruct laminar airflow

## Organization Tips

- Store by temperature: room temp, refrigerated, frozen separated
- Label shelves: Media, supplements, antibiotics, buffers, etc.
- Keep high-use bottles at eye level for quick access
- Place expiration dates on spine labels facing out
- Dedicate one section to quality control and standards

## Installation

Simply place in the BSC. No adhesives or drilling required. For tall cabinets, stack multiple racks and use interlocking feet to prevent shifting.
