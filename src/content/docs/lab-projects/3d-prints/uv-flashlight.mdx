A ruggedized housing for a compact UV LED module, designed for lab use including gel visualization, surface sterilization, and culture inspection. The enclosure protects the LED electronics while providing an ergonomic handheld form factor.

## Design

The housing integrates a standard 365nm or 405nm UV LED module with protective optics and a reflector that concentrates light output. The design includes ventilation slots for thermal management during extended use. An optional magnetic base allows hands-free positioning or wall mounting near common work areas.

## Hardware

- **LED module:** 365nm or 405nm UV LED (10-20W typical)
- **Housing:** 3D-printed impact-resistant PETG
- **Lens:** acrylic or polycarbonate lens for beam shaping
- **Reflector:** aluminum-coated interior cavity
- **Power:** USB-C input with integrated buck converter (5V to 12V)
- **Switch:** momentary or toggle switch for user control

## Features

- **Protective:** impact-resistant design survives lab drops
- **Ergonomic:** comfortable grip for extended handheld use
- **Ventilated:** cooling fins and slots prevent LED thermal shutdown
- **Compact:** fits in lab coat pocket, minimal storage space
- **Magnetic base:** optional mount for hands-free positioning
- **Durable:** PETG material resists UV damage and chemical splash

## Applications

- **Gel visualization:** DNA/RNA staining inspection
- **Surface sterilization:** BSC cleaning and decontamination
- **Culture inspection:** detecting bioluminescent organisms
- **PCR verification:** checking for amplification glow
- **Troubleshooting:** finding contamination or growth patterns

## Safety

UV light is hazardous to eyes and skin. The housing includes a lens to diffuse and filter exposure. Always use appropriate UV-protective eyewear when operating.
