A simple but robust clip designed to securely mount tape dispensers to lab benches and biosafety cabinet frames. Keeps autoclave tape, color tape, and labeling tape within arm's reach without consuming valuable work surface.

## Design

The clip uses a reinforced C-clamp mechanism to grip edge surfaces. The design accommodates standard tape dispenser widths and includes a quick-release toggle for easy cartridge changes. Corner reinforcement prevents flex under tension.

## Hardware

- **Material:** 3D-printed PETG or reinforced nylon
- **Clamp mechanism:** Screw-tightened or snap-fit toggle
- **Grip range:** typically 0.5" - 1" thick edges
- **Cartridge mount:** universal interface for standard dispensers

## Features

- **Secure hold:** won't slip during tape pulling
- **Quick-release:** toggle-lever for fast tape cartridge swaps
- **Compact:** minimal footprint on benches or cabinet frames
- **Reinforced:** won't crack under repeated use
- **Adjustable:** works on various edge thicknesses

## Mounting Options

- **Bench edge:** C-clamp to work surface edge
- **BSC frame:** clamp to cabinet side rail
- **Adhesive:** optional sticky base for non-critical surfaces

## Tape Types

Works with:
- Autoclave tape (steam sterilization indicator)
- Color tape (standard laboratory labeling)
- Masking tape (temporary labels)
- Painter's tape (temporary protection)

## Installation

1. Loosen the clamp screw fully
2. Position dispenser at desired height
3. Tighten clamp screw gradually until snug (don't over-tighten)
4. Confirm dispenser doesn't shift during normal tape pulling
