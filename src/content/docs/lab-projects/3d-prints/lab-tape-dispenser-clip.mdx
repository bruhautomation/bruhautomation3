A clip to keep your lab tape dispenser within reach on the bench — mounts with a magnet or adhesive.

Lab tape has a way of disappearing — someone borrows the roll and it never comes back. This clip parks a tape dispenser in one spot within arm's reach of your bench or fume hood, so it's always where you left it. It mounts two ways: a magnet for steel surfaces and equipment frames, or an adhesive backing for anything non-magnetic. Print it, mount it, and drop your dispenser in.

## Mounting and printing

Print in PLA or PETG, no supports. For the magnetic version, the model has a pocket for a small disc magnet — glue it in with a drop of superglue or epoxy before mounting. For the adhesive version, clean the surface first and use a strong mounting tape (VHB-style) so the hold survives daily use. The clip is sized around a standard handheld lab-tape dispenser; adjust the width in the STEP file if yours is chunkier.
