A purpose-built tube rack designed specifically for the CEDEX BioHT benchtop analyzer. Holds 24 sample tubes in the exact configuration expected by the instrument's autosampler, reducing sample preparation time and eliminating transfer errors.

## Design

The rack positions sample tubes in two organized columns with clear numeric labeling. The geometry matches the CEDEX BioHT autosampler footprint exactly, allowing samples to be loaded once and carried directly to the instrument. Built-in stop blocks prevent accidental tube ejection.

## Hardware

- **Material:** High-temperature autoclavable plastic (polycarbonate or PETG)
- **Capacity:** 24 tubes (12 per column)
- **Tube compatibility:** Standard 12-15mL conical or round-bottom tubes
- **Dimensions:** ~4" W × 10" H (matches CEDEX deck footprint)
- **Weight:** lightweight for bench handling

## Features

- **CEDEX-compatible:** matches autosampler dimensions exactly
- **24-tube capacity:** full run without sample reloading
- **Numeric labels:** 1-24 for tracking and method setup
- **Autoclavable:** 121°C sterilization compatible
- **Secure positioning:** tubes seated firmly, no rattle
- **Quick orientation:** clear front/back indication

## Workflow

1. Prepare 24 samples in standard tubes
2. Load samples into rack in numerical order (1-24)
3. Place rack in CEDEX autosampler
4. Select "Tube positions 1-24" in instrument software
5. Run analysis without re-positioning

## Compatibility

- **Instrument:** CEDEX BioHT (Roche/BIOQUELL)
- **Tubes:** Falcon, Corning, or equivalent 12-15mL conical
- **Temperature:** Supports chilled samples (refrigerated samples OK up to sampling)
