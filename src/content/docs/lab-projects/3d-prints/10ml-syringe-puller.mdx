A motorized syringe puller jig that enables accurate, repeatable liquid transfers for cell culture work. Replaces manual syringe operation with microstepped motor control for consistent withdrawal and dispensing.

## Design

A NEMA 17 stepper motor drives a lead screw that pulls the plunger of a standard 10mL syringe. The 3D-printed jig holds the syringe barrel in a fixed position while the motor carriage moves the plunger at controlled rates. This allows precise volume dispensing without the hand coordination required for manual pipetting.

## Hardware

- **Motor:** NEMA 17 stepper
- **Driver:** A4988 microstepping driver
- **Mechanism:** Lead screw with printed nut block
- **Frame:** 3D-printed PETG or carbon fiber reinforced base
- **Syringe holder:** Quick-connect 10mL barrel clamp
- **Plunger drive:** Printed plunger carriage with lead screw coupling

## Features

- **Micro-volume control:** microlitre resolution
- **Repeatable:** consistent volumes across transfers
- **Universal syringes:** works with standard Luer lock 10mL syringes
- **Compact benchtop size:** fits beside incubators and media stations
- **Programmable:** adjust pull/dispense rates via firmware
- **Simple:** minimal assembly, standard fasteners

## Applications

- Precise media transfers
- Feeding cell cultures
- Sample collection
- Reagent additions
- Cell passaging
