A simple 3D printed jig for pulling syringe plungers with consistent force — useful for microfluidics work and sample preparation.

Drawing a plunger back by hand is fiddly and inconsistent: the plunger creeps, or you can't hold a steady pull while your other hand is busy. This jig holds the syringe and lets you draw the plunger to a repeatable position, so you get the same volume and the same steady pull every time. It's a print-and-use part — no hardware and no assembly. Print it, drop in your syringe, and go.

## Printing and fit

Prints fine in PLA or PETG on any FDM machine, no supports needed — lay it flat on the bed so the plunger channel comes out clean. It's sized around a standard 10 mL Luer-lock syringe; if your syringe barrel runs a little larger or smaller, scale the model a percent or two, or open the STEP file and adjust the bore before printing. Do a quick test fit before you rely on it for real samples.
