A high-capacity storage rack for 15mL centrifuge tubes that keeps hundreds of samples organized, accessible, and vertically integrated. Perfect for bioproduction labs, tissue culture facilities, and sample banks where space is premium and inventory management is critical.

## Design

The rack features a dense grid of holes sized for 15mL conical tubes (Falcon compatible). The design incorporates a slight backward angle for gravity-assisted tube retention and ergonomic access. Individual slots are numbered for inventory tracking, and the modular design allows stacking for additional capacity without expanding floor footprint.

## Features

- **Capacity:** 100+ tubes in a compact 2-3 ft² footprint
- **Durable:** PETG or reinforced nylon recommended for lab environment
- **Angled design:** 15-20° tilt aids access and prevents tube rollout
- **Stackable:** interlocking feet and alignment pins enable safe vertical stacking
- **Numbered slots:** built-in inventory tracking
- **Autoclavable:** high-temperature tolerant material selection

## Materials

- **Print material:** PETG or medical-grade nylon (resistant to ethanol, chemical splash)
- **Print settings:** 100% infill for rigidity
- **Post-processing:** autoclave-safe without warping

## Footprint

- **Base dimensions:** ~12" × 18" (variable depending on layout)
- **Height per rack:** ~8"
- **Stackable height for 3 racks:** ~24" (fits most shelf systems)

## Organization

Number each row and column for quick sample location. Integrate with LIMS (Laboratory Information Management System) for digital tracking, or use printed labels for manual inventory.
