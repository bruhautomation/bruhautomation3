A precision liquid dispensing system for automated culture feeding, reagent addition, and sample collection. Uses a stepper-driven peristaltic pump head capable of microlitre to millilitre deliveries with ±2% accuracy.

## Design

The pump consists of a NEMA 17 stepper motor connected to a peristaltic pump head through a direct drive coupling. The stepper's microstepping capability enables fine control down to microliter volumes. The system connects to a 12mm silicone tube, allowing flexible routing through bioreactor port plates and incubator walls.

## Hardware

- **Motor:** NEMA 17 stepper motor (12V, 1.68A)
- **Pump head:** Peristaltic pump (12mm tubing, standard roller configuration)
- **Driver:** A4988 microstepping driver
- **Controller:** ESP32 or Arduino with MQTT/Home Assistant integration
- **Tubing:** Medical-grade silicone (12mm OD, 8mm ID)
- **Connectors:** Quick-disconnect Luer fittings (sterile capable)

## Features

- **Precision dispensing:** ±2% accuracy after calibration
- **Range:** 0.5 µL/step to multi-millilitre per cycle
- **Reversible:** dispense and retract with same command
- **Programmable:** standalone firmware or integrated with HA/MQTT
- **Calibration:** simple per-system calibration based on actual output

## Calibration

Measure the actual volume dispensed for 100 motor steps at your target speed. Typical volumes are 0.5-2 µL/step depending on tube diameter and roller configuration. Update the firmware with your µL/step ratio for accurate dosing.

## Installation

Mount on DIN rail or directly to bioreactor frame. The pump head sits at incubator height with flexible tubing routed to media bottles and reactor ports. Use inline check valves to prevent backflow.
