An automated 96-well plate inversion mechanism for unattended cell culture work. Inverts plates precisely for media exchange, antibody washes, or automated retrieval sequences. Holds plates securely during rotation and maintains stable inverted position.

## Design

A NEMA 17 stepper motor drives a motorized gripper that holds a 96-well plate perpendicular to the bench surface. Smooth microstepping allows controlled 180° rotation with programmable dwell times. The gripper design accommodates standard tissue culture plates with precise edge engagement.

The 3D-printed gripper arms are reinforced with aluminum inserts for durability. The plate is held firmly during inversion and positioned at detents to prevent spillage during media transfers.

## Hardware

- **Motor:** NEMA 17 stepper (precise positioning control)
- **Driver:** A4988 microstepping driver
- **Controller:** Arduino or ESP32 with RS232 or MQTT interface
- **Frame:** 40mm aluminum extrusion or 3D-printed PETG base
- **Gripper:** 3D-printed with brass inserts for wear resistance
- **Plate holder:** Adjustable for various plate styles

## Features

- **Smooth rotation:** microstepped motor for vibration-free operation
- **Safety:** mechanical detents and position feedback prevent spillage
- **Adjustable dwell:** 1-60 second inverted hold times via firmware
- **Speed control:** programmable rotation speed
- **Universal fit:** works with standard 96-well and smaller plate formats
- **Compact:** fits on standard lab benches near incubators

## How It Works

The stepper holds the plate in vertical position. Upon command, it smoothly rotates 180°, holds the plate inverted for the programmed duration, then returns to upright. Position sensors confirm safe plate seating before releasing the gripper.

## Integration

Pairs with peristaltic pumps and automated pipetting systems for fully integrated microplate workflows. MQTT integration allows orchestration with Home Assistant or custom lab automation platforms.
