Sonoff S31 smart plug with integrated CSE7766 power metering for real-time current, voltage, and wattage monitoring. Control your plugged-in devices remotely and track energy consumption via Home Assistant.

## How It Works

The S31 is a standard outlet plug with built-in ESP8266, relay, and power meter. The CSE7766 chip measures voltage, current, and power consumption via UART. A physical button toggles the relay locally, while Home Assistant provides remote control and energy tracking. The status LED shows relay state.

## Hardware

- **Board:** ESP8266 (esp01_1m, 1MB flash)
- **Power Meter:** CSE7766 (UART at 4800 baud, even parity)
- **Relay:** 16A AC rated (GPIO12)
- **LED:** Status indicator (GPIO13)
- **Button:** Physical control (GPIO0)
- **Ranges:** 85-260V AC, 0-16A, 0-3680W
