Sonoff S20 smart plug offering simple relay control and LED indicator without power metering. Ideal for controlling basic on/off devices and automation, from lights to heaters to fans.

## How It Works

The S20 is a simple outlet plug with an ESP8266, relay, and status LED. Unlike the S31, it has no power monitoring—just straightforward relay control. A physical button toggles the device locally, while Home Assistant provides remote switching and automation triggers.

## Hardware

- **Board:** ESP8266 (esp01_1m, 1MB flash)
- **Relay:** 16A AC rated (GPIO12)
- **LED:** Status indicator (GPIO13)
- **Button:** Physical control (GPIO0)
- **Power Rating:** 3680W max (16A @ 230V AC)
