UV monitoring device with LTR390 UV sensor, BME280 environmental sensor, and SSD1306 OLED display. Track UV index, ambient light levels, temperature, humidity, and pressure in real-time.

## How It Works

Two I2C buses on the ESP32 handle the LTR390 UV/light sensor and BME280 environmental sensor independently. The OLED displays live readings and trends. Delta filtering reduces noise while keeping data responsive. Perfect for outdoor use or UV exposure tracking.

## Hardware

- **Board:** ESP32 (ESP32-Dev)
- **UV Sensor:** LTR390 (UV index and ambient light)
- **Environmental:** BME280 (temperature, humidity, pressure)
- **Display:** SSD1306 OLED 128×64
- **I2C:** Two separate buses (bus_a and bus_b)

## Firmware
