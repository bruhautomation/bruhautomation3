M5Stack Atom Echo based voice assistant with native Home Assistant integration. Control your smart home with voice commands and automations without relying on cloud services.

## How It Works

The M5Stack Atom Echo is a compact ESP32 cube with integrated I2S microphone, speaker, and RGB LED. ESPHome configures it as a local voice assistant that listens for your voice, processes commands through Home Assistant, and speaks responses back. The RGB LED provides visual feedback during listening, processing, and error states.

## Hardware

- **Microcontroller:** M5Stack Atom Echo (ESP32-based, fully integrated)
- **Audio:** Built-in I2S microphone and amplified speaker
- **Status:** Single addressable RGB LED
- **Button:** Capacitive touch for voice trigger and controls
- **Size:** Compact 24×24mm cube

## Firmware

This project runs [ESPHome](https://esphome.io/). The full configuration is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/bruhautomation/BRUH-CAD-Files/tree/main/smart-home/voice-assistant).
