Motorized blinds using a servo motor with capacitive touch control, NeoPixel LED feedback, and full Home Assistant integration for automated window management. Open, close, or tilt to any position.

## How It Works

A servo motor drives a mechanical linkage that opens and closes the blind rod. The ESP32's integrated capacitive touch button controls the blinds locally (single tap opens, long press closes), while Home Assistant can also trigger any position. NeoPixel LEDs provide visual feedback. The servo detaches after each movement to minimize power consumption.

## Hardware

- **Board:** ESP32 (NodeMCU-32S)
- **Actuator:** Servo motor (PWM on GPIO at 50 Hz)
- **Control:** Capacitive touch sensor (native ESP32)
- **Feedback:** 24-LED NeoPixel strip (addressable RGB)
- **Power:** Servo draws 500mA under load, idle servo draws nothing

## Firmware

This project runs [ESPHome](https://esphome.io/). The full configuration is available on [GitHub](https://github.com/bruhautomation/BRUH-CAD-Files/tree/main/smart-home/smart-blinds).
