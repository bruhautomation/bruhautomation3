## Hey, I'm Ben

I started BRUH Automation in 2015 to help make smart home tech accessible to regular people. This site is where I document my projects — from connected devices to random maker builds to biotech lab tools. Everything here is open-source, and most of it includes 3D print files you can download and remix.

## About This Site

I paused the BRUH Automation YouTube channel a while back. Balancing content creation with everything else was tough, and I wanted anything I put out to actually be useful — not just noise. YouTube's algorithm also isn't great for evergreen how-to content.

This site is my way of keeping things going at my own pace. It's easier to maintain, easier to update, and doesn't punish you for watching a video from 2016.
