## About

I started BRUH Automation in 2015 to make smart home tech accessible to everyone. Everything here is open-source — from connected devices to maker builds to biotech lab tools — with 3D print files you can download and remix.

I paused the YouTube channel a while back. I wanted anything I put out to actually be useful, not just noise. This site is my way of keeping things going at my own pace.
