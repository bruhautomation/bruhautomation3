## About This Site

I started BRUH Automation in 2015 to make smart home tech accessible to everyone. Here you'll find open-source projects — from connected devices to maker builds to biotech lab tools — with 3D print files you can download and remix.

I paused the YouTube channel a while back — I wanted anything I put out to actually be useful, not just noise. This site is my way of keeping things going at my own pace. It's easier to maintain, easier to update, and doesn't punish you for watching a video from 2016.
